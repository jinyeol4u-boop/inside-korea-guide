Myeongdong Shopping Guide: What to Buy and Where

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 7, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Take Line 4 to Myeongdong Station (Exits 5 to 10) and walk the two main shopping streets for Korean cosmetics, fashion and department stores. Hours vary by shop; Olive Young Myeongdong Town lists 10:00 to 22:30. Spend 15,000 won or more at a tax refund shop with your passport to claim roughly 5 to 8% back.

What should I know before I go?

Details checked November 7, 2026

Myeongdong is open daily, but every shop sets its own hours, so check the store you care about before you travel. Visit Seoul describes it as Seoul's main shopping area, a mix of street stalls and retail stores, and says it is a commercial, banking and cultural hub with a daytime population of 1.5 to 2 million people.

  • Worth knowing: According to VisitKorea, two main streets meet at the centre of the block, one starting at Myeong-dong Station and the other at Lotte Department Store in Euljiro.
  • A common mistake: Assuming one set of opening hours. Visit Seoul lists no general hours, and the shops on the VisitKorea store list range from 10:00 to 22:30 down to 11:00 to 20:00.
  • Local tip: Bring your physical passport. Tax refund shops ask for it, and our tax refund guide explains the rules.

What can I buy in Myeongdong?

Mainly Korean cosmetics and clothing, plus shoes, accessories and everyday items. Visit Seoul says the area sells everything from high fashion to casual wear and many Korean cosmetics stores, and calls prices relatively affordable compared with Apgujeong-dong and Cheongdam-dong, though it gives no price figures.

  • Cosmetics: Brand shops and beauty chains line the streets. VisitKorea's store list includes a Laneige showroom on Myeong-dong 8-gil and the Olive Young Myeongdong Town store.
  • Fashion: VisitKorea notes that, unlike Namdaemun or Dongdaemun, many designer brands are sold here, and its list includes a women's shoe shop, Sappun Myeongdong Stage.
  • Department stores: VisitKorea names Lotte, Shinsegae, Myeong-dong Migliore, Noon Square and M Plaza.
  • Everyday goods: Daiso's Myeongdong main store is described as a 12-story shop with fashion, stationery, kitchen and household goods.

Practical example: a visitor who wants skincare gifts, one pair of shoes and a rainy-day indoor stop can combine a beauty store, Sappun and Lotte without leaving the same few streets.

How does the tax refund work here?

Only some shops offer it, so look for the tax refund mark and ask before you pay. The VisitKorea list above shows that Olive Young, Sappun and Daiso offer a refund while Laneige and ROSA.K do not, even though they sit on the same streets.

  • Minimum spend: Our guide gives 15,000 won per receipt, with an immediate in-shop refund under 1,000,000 won per purchase. Rules change, so confirm in the tax refund guide and with shop staff.
  • Bring your passport: Keep the receipt and the unopened goods for customs if you claim at the airport.
  • Need help? VisitKorea lists the 1330 Travel Hotline (+82-2-1330) in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese.

How do I plan a Myeongdong shopping trip?

Arrive at Myeongdong Station, shop the beauty streets first, then move to the department stores and finish by checking your receipts.

  1. Choose your exit. Take Line 4 to Myeongdong Station and use an exit between 5 and 10, as Visit Seoul advises.
  2. Check store hours. Look up the shops you want, because opening times differ and some close early.
  3. Shop beauty and fashion streets. Walk Myeong-dong-gil and Myeong-dong 8-gil, where the VisitKorea-listed shops sit.
  4. Ask about tax refunds at the counter. Show your passport before paying and keep each receipt.
  5. Store your bags if needed. VisitKorea lists T-luggage service on the underground first floor of the station, near Exits 9 and 10.
  6. Add a meal. Dinner options include the evening stalls in our Myeongdong street food guide.

FAQ

What is Myeongdong best known for shopping?

Korean cosmetics, clothing from high fashion to casual wear, and department stores, according to Visit Seoul and VisitKorea.

Which subway exit should I use for Myeongdong?

Myeongdong Station on Line 4, Exits 5 to 10, per Visit Seoul. Pick the exit closest to the street you want.

Can I get a tax refund in Myeongdong?

Yes, at shops that display a tax refund mark. VisitKorea shows Olive Young, Sappun and Daiso as offering it and Laneige and ROSA.K as not, so ask before paying.

Where can I leave my bags while I shop?

VisitKorea lists a T-luggage service on the underground first floor of Myeong-dong Station near Exits 9 and 10. Confirm hours and prices there.
