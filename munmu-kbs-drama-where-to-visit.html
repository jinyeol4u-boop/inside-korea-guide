Munmu: KBS's Silla Epic Drama and Where to Visit

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 21, 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

Munmu is a 28-episode KBS 2TV historical epic that airs Saturdays and Sundays at 9:20 p.m. Korea time from November 14, 2026. It follows Silla's King Munmu. Fans can visit his sea tomb, Gameunsa Temple Site and a new museum in east Gyeongju, plus drama sets in Mungyeong.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 21, 2026

The places tied to Munmu are spread across two regions: the historical sites are on Gyeongju's east coast, and the filming sets are in Mungyeong, in the north-west of the same province. Pick one per day unless you have a car.

  • Worth knowing: The museum near the tomb is new. Gyeongju City held its completion ceremony in March 2026, and its official page says admission is temporarily free until December 31, 2026.
  • A common mistake: Expecting to walk to the tomb. Daewangam is a rock in the sea, viewed from Bonggil Beach; it is not open to visitors.
  • Local tip: The east coast is a classic New Year sunrise spot. Gyeongju City's webzine describes the tomb area filling with crowds on January 1, so expect traffic that morning.

What is the Munmu drama about?

Munmu follows Kim Beop-min, the Silla prince who became King Munmu, through the 7th-century wars between Silla, Goguryeo, Baekje and Tang China. It is KBS's 35th "daeha" (grand historical) drama and its first in about three years, following The Goryeo-Khitan War, according to The Fact and Sports Kyunghyang.

The first two episodes set up Kim Chun-chu's struggle to survive Silla's court politics and Yeon Gaesomun's coup in Goguryeo, which overthrew King Yeongnyu, as summarised by Ilgan Sports. Korean press reports describe a budget of about 29.4 billion won, the largest for a KBS drama, and battle scenes made with the help of AI technology. Some English streaming guides list the show under other titles, such as Sword & Silence, so search for "Munmu" or the Korean title 문무.

How can I watch Munmu?

In Korea, watch it live on KBS 2TV or stream it on Netflix Korea and Wavve; abroad, availability depends on your country.

  • In Korea: Ilgan Sports reported it ranked first on both Netflix's Korean series chart and Wavve on November 17, 2026, which confirms both services carry it locally.
  • United States: The streaming guide Reelgood lists KOCOWA+ as the US home for the series, with English subtitles. This is a secondary source; confirm on KOCOWA+ itself.
  • Elsewhere: Search your Netflix, KOCOWA+ or local streaming app for "Munmu". Avoid sites that ask you to install a VPN or unknown player.
  • Episode timing: At two episodes a weekend, the 28 episodes take about 14 weeks, so the finale falls in early 2027 unless KBS changes the schedule for holidays or sports.

Who was the real King Munmu?

King Munmu was Silla's 30th king, ruling from 661 to 681, and he completed the unification of most of the Korean Peninsula after driving out Tang forces in 676. He was the son of Kim Chun-chu, who became King Taejong Muyeol, Silla's 29th king.

  • The sea tomb: He asked to be buried in the East Sea so he could become a dragon and protect the kingdom. Gyeongju City's museum page describes the tomb as lying off Bonggil-ri, in keeping with his wish.
  • Gameunsa Temple Site: Munmu began the temple to guard the coast, and his son King Sinmun completed it. Two three-story stone pagodas still stand there.
  • Igyeondae: A pavilion built by King Sinmun overlooking the tomb, about a three-minute walk from the new museum.
  • The year 676: The museum's courtyard monument is 6.76 m tall to mark the year unification was completed, according to the city's webzine.

Practical example: watching episodes with this timeline in mind helps; the drama is a dramatisation, and our K-drama section will add an episode-by-episode history guide later in the season.

How do I visit King Munmu's sites in Gyeongju?

Base yourself at the King Munmu Maritime History Museum in Gampo-eup; the tomb and Gameunsa are about five minutes away by car, and Igyeondae is a short walk.

  • Address: 1473 Donghaean-ro, Gampo-eup, Gyeongju, per the museum's directions page.
  • By bus: From the Gyeongju Express Bus Terminal stop, take bus 150 (or 150-1 or 1150), get off at Daejonggyo and walk about 10 minutes.
  • From central Gyeongju: The museum lists about 35 minutes by car from Hwangnidan-gil, Daereungwon and Bomun.
  • Inside the museum: The second-floor media room screens a film on unification, the Battle of Gibeolpo and Munmu's legacy three times an hour, at :00, :20 and :40, per the exhibition page.
  • Closed days: Mondays, January 1, and Seollal and Chuseok days.

Getting to Gyeongju itself takes about 2 hours by KTX from Seoul to Singyeongju Station; our Gyeongju one-day guide covers that leg.

Where was Munmu filmed, and can I visit?

Mungyeong in North Gyeongsang Province is the main filming base, and its Saejae Open Set is open to visitors for 2,000 won. Kyungbuk Maeil reported on November 17, 2026 that the drama features the Mungyeong Saejae Open Set, the Gaeun and Maseong open sets, the Saejae pass and Gomosanseong Fortress.

  • Mungyeong Saejae Open Set: 932 Saejae-ro, Mungyeong. VisitKorea-based listings give admission of 2,000 won for adults, 1,000 won for teens and 500 won for children, open year-round except January 1, Seollal and Chuseok. Confirm on the Mungyeong tourism site.
  • The Saejae trail: The provincial park around the set is free, with a dirt path linking three old gates.
  • Getting there: KTX-Eum trains run from Pangyo Station (south of Seoul) to Mungyeong Station in about 90 minutes, according to Korean reports from the line's opening in late 2024. Only a few trains run each way per day, so book early on Korail, then take a local bus or taxi to the park.
  • Not verified: I found no official list of which Munmu scenes used which set, and the Gaeun and Maseong sets have separate access rules.

Practical example: the Saejae set also served the 2026 hit film about King Danjong and many Joseon dramas, so one visit covers several productions; our Seoul K-drama locations guide covers spots closer to the capital.

How do I plan a Munmu day trip?

Do east Gyeongju as a half day from the city, and Mungyeong as a separate day trip from Seoul.

  1. Check the museum day: Avoid Mondays, and remember winter closing is 17:00.
  2. Morning, Gyeongju centre: Take bus 150 from the express bus terminal area toward Gampo.
  3. Midday, museum and Igyeondae: Watch the media film, then walk to the pavilion for the tomb view.
  4. Afternoon, tomb and Gameunsa: View Daewangam from Bonggil Beach and see the twin pagodas; a taxi saves time between them.
  5. Another day, Mungyeong: Ride KTX-Eum from Pangyo, walk the set and the first gate of the Saejae trail.

FAQ

When does Munmu air and how many episodes are there?

KBS 2TV airs Munmu every Saturday and Sunday at 9:20 p.m. Korea time. It premiered on November 14, 2026 and has 28 episodes, so it runs into early 2027.

Where can I watch Munmu outside Korea?

Availability depends on your country. Inside Korea it streamed at number one on Netflix and Wavve on November 17, 2026, and the streaming guide Reelgood lists KOCOWA+ in the United States. Check your local services.

Is King Munmu's underwater tomb real?

Daewangam is a rocky islet just off Bonggil Beach in Gyeongju that tradition and the city identify as the sea tomb of King Munmu, who asked to be buried in the East Sea. You view it from the beach; you cannot land on it.

Is the King Munmu Maritime History Museum free?

Its official page lists adult admission at 3,000 won but also says entry is temporarily free until December 31, 2026. It closes on Mondays, and winter hours are 10:00 to 17:00.

Can I visit the Munmu filming sets?

Yes. Local reports say scenes were shot at the Mungyeong Saejae Open Set, the Gaeun and Maseong open sets and Gomosanseong Fortress in Mungyeong. The Saejae set charges 2,000 won for adults.
