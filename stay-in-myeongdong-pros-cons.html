Staying in Myeongdong: Pros, Cons and Who It Suits

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 16, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Stay in Myeongdong if you want shopping, food and subway access on your doorstep. Line 4 Myeongdong Station (exits 5 to 10) and Line 2 Euljiro 1(il)-ga serve it, and airport bus 6015 stops at the station. Expect crowds and lively evenings, so light sleepers should look at quieter areas.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked October 16, 2026

Myeongdong is a compact, busy shopping district in Jung-gu, so your exact street and exit matter more than the neighborhood name. Visit Seoul gives the subway access as Line 4, Myeongdong Station, exits 5 to 10, and says the area has a daytime population of 1.5 to 2 million.

  • Worth knowing: Visit Seoul's editors also list Euljiro 1(il)-ga Station (Line 2) as access, which gives you two lines instead of one.
  • A common mistake: Booking by neighborhood name only. Measure the walk from the nearest station exit to the hotel, especially with luggage.
  • Local tip: The Myeongdong Tourist Information Center and the 1330 Korea Travel Hotline (Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese, per VisitKorea) can help if plans change.

Is Myeongdong a good place to stay in Seoul?

Yes, if shopping, food and easy transit matter more to you than quiet. VisitKorea describes Myeong-dong as one of the primary shopping districts in Seoul, with department stores such as Lotte, Shinsegae, Myeong-dong Migliore, Noon Square and M Plaza.

Pros

  • Shopping and cosmetics nearby. Visit Seoul calls it Seoul's shopping hub, known for Korean cosmetics stores, and VisitKorea adds hair salons, banks and theaters to the mix.
  • Food at every hour of the day. VisitKorea notes many restaurants specialize in dongaseu (pork cutlet) and kalguksu (noodle soup), and Visit Seoul describes street stalls selling skewers, tornado potatoes and crepes on both sides of the main street.
  • A central base. Visit Seoul's editors call Myeongdong a good starting point for reaching Gangnam, Dongdaemun or Hongdae.
  • Sights on foot. Myeongdong Cathedral, built in 1898, is in the district; the page lists hours of 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and 7:00 to 22:00 on weekends, but warns that details come from past data.

Cons

  • Crowds. Visit Seoul says foreign visitors have recently crowded the area, and reviewers on its page mention large crowds during spring holidays.
  • Not a quiet neighborhood. The area is a commercial and entertainment hub, so choose a room away from the main street if you are a light sleeper (a practical suggestion, not an official rating).
  • A commercial feel. Visit Seoul describes the district as a hub of commerce, banking and culture, which suits a short, busy stay better than a quiet, local-style one (our judgment).

Practical example: a four-night shopping trip works well here, because stores, a food street and a cathedral are all within a short walk of a Line 4 station.

How do I get to Myeongdong from Incheon Airport?

Take the AREX to Seoul Station and change to Line 4, or check airport bus route 6015, which airport.kr lists as serving Myeongdong Station. VisitKorea's airport guide gives AREX times to Seoul Station of about 43 minutes (Express) and 59 minutes (all-stop) from Terminal 1, and about 51 and 66 minutes from Terminal 2.

Who should stay in Myeongdong, and who should not?

Choose Myeongdong for shopping and short, busy trips; choose Hongdae, Insadong or Seoul Station for nightlife, tradition or onward trains. Our comparison of Myeongdong, Hongdae and Insadong covers the day-time differences in more detail.

How do I choose a hotel in Myeongdong?

Pick the station exit first, then the hotel, and check the walk and the room's street side. Visit Seoul's hotel listings include Myeongdong as an area filter.

  1. Pick your exit. Choose a property within a short walk of a Myeongdong Station exit (5 to 10) or Euljiro 1(il)-ga Station.
  2. Plan the airport trip. Decide between the AREX plus Line 4 route and bus 6015, then check the walk from the stop with luggage.
  3. Ask about noise. Request a room away from the main street or on an upper floor.
  4. Check the cancellation rules. Confirm the policy before you pay, and read current guest reviews on the booking site.

FAQ

Is Myeongdong a good area to stay in Seoul for first-timers?

Yes for shopping-focused trips. VisitKorea calls it one of the primary shopping districts in Seoul, and Myeongdong Station on Line 4 and Euljiro 1(il)-ga Station on Line 2 connect it to the rest of the city.

How do I get from Incheon Airport to Myeongdong?

Take the AREX to Seoul Station and change to Line 4, or check airport bus route 6015 (Myeongdong Stn), which airport.kr lists. Confirm fares and times on airport.kr.

Is Myeongdong crowded?

Yes. Visit Seoul says foreign visitors have recently crowded the area, and its daytime population is 1.5 to 2 million, so expect busy streets, especially in the evening.

Is there a late-night airport bus to Myeongdong?

Not directly. airport.kr's late-night timetable for Terminal 1 does not name Myeongdong, though route N6701 stops at Euljiro Entrance about 65 minutes after departure; verify before you rely on it.
