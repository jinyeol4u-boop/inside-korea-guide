Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond at Night: Gyeongju Guide

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 8, 2026 · 7 min read

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 8, 2026

Go in the early evening, buy a ticket before the last entry, and treat the reported fees as something to confirm at the gate. Sources differ on ticket prices, and the site is busiest around sunset.

  • Worth knowing: VisitKorea lists operating hours of 09:00 to 22:00 and says the site is especially attractive at night. A Korea Trip Tips listing gives last ticketing and admission at 21:30 and says entry may be restricted near closing time because of crowds.
  • A common mistake: Arriving just before closing. Because last admission is reported as 21:30 and entry can be limited when it is crowded, aim to be at the ticket gate well before then.
  • Local tip: The same listing says interpretation services are not offered, so read the signs on the VisitKorea page in advance and keep a translation app ready.

What are Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond?

It is the royal pleasure garden of the Silla kingdom, built in 674 and later known as Anapji. Wikipedia gives 674 as the construction year under King Munmu, lists it as Historic Site No. 18, and describes an oval pond roughly 200 m east to west and 180 m north to south with three small islands.

VisitKorea explains that princes stayed at the palace and that it hosted celebrations of national events and receptions for foreign guests. After the Silla dynasty fell, the site was abandoned for centuries and earned the name Anapji from a poet's lament about wild geese and ducks. Excavation began in 1975 and recovered about 24,000 relics, including a piece of earthenware engraved with the name Wolji, which let the site regain its original name.

The area is part of the Gyeongju Historic Areas, which VisitKorea lists as a UNESCO World Heritage site. According to Wikipedia, about 730 relics are on display at the Anapji Exhibition Hall in the Gyeongju National Museum.

Practical example: if you have already seen the museum by day, a short evening at the pond lets you compare the excavated objects with the garden they came from.

When is it open and how much does it cost?

It is open daily from 09:00 to 22:00, and one listing gives 3,000 won for adults, with last admission at 21:30. Another regional site shows lower prices, so the figures below are approximate.

Why visit at night, and what time is best?

The buildings are lit from dusk and reflected in the still water, so the best window is from sunset into the evening. VisitKorea says the lights around Imhaejeon Hall and the old buildings create the reflection that makes the pond so popular after dark.

  • Dusk to early evening: One travel guide advises arriving at sunset and staying until the crowds thin, and says the reconstructed buildings are lit from dusk.
  • Later in the evening: TRVLOGUE says the night view is strongest after sunset, when the illuminated pavilions reflect on the water. It does not give an exact time, so use the last admission as your limit.
  • Season: Sunset comes in the late afternoon in November, which means you can see the lights without a late night. Check the sunset time on your weather app for your date.

Practical example: arriving about 30 minutes before sunset lets you walk the pond in daylight, then watch the lights come on and take a second lap.

How do I get to Wolji Pond?

Walk or cycle from central Gyeongju, or take a taxi or city bus and confirm the route in a map app. No official page checked for this article gives a bus number to Wolji Pond.

  • On foot from Hwangnidan-gil: TRVLOGUE gives about 20 minutes. VisitKorea's Gyeongju course lists Hwangnidan-gil, Daereungwon, Woljeonggyo Bridge and Wolji Pond as stops one to four.
  • Walking between sights: The travel guide says Wolji Pond, Tumuli Park, Cheomseongdae and the National Museum are within walking distance of each other once you reach the centre.
  • By bicycle: The same guide reports bike rental near Gyeongju Station at 5,000 to 10,000 won per day. Our Gyeongju bike route guide shows a loop that includes Wolji Pond.
  • By bus: The guide says city buses connect Gyeongju Station and Singyeongju Station to the main attractions, with English route information posted at both stations. Its bus numbers are tied to Bulguksa, not Wolji Pond.
  • By car: Parking is available, and TRVLOGUE warns traffic gets congested on weekends and in peak seasons.

If you are coming from Seoul or Busan, see our guide to booking KTX tickets and read about Bulguksa and Seokguram for a daytime plan on the same trip.

What does a good evening route look like?

Walk from Hwangnidan-gil to the pond at dusk, cross Woljeonggyo Bridge for the night view, and leave before the last admission rush. This sample uses the order on VisitKorea's course and the timing reported above.

  1. Start in Hwangnidan-gil in the late afternoon. VisitKorea describes it as an area where hanok buildings blend with trendy shops.
  2. Pass the Daereungwon tomb complex. VisitKorea lists it as stop two on its course.
  3. Reach Wolji Pond near sunset. Buy your ticket and walk the pond edge once in the remaining light.
  4. Stay as the lights come on. Look for the reflections of the buildings, which VisitKorea highlights.
  5. Cross Woljeonggyo Bridge. VisitKorea calls it renowned for its night views and lists it as stop three on the course.
  6. Head out before closing. Allow 30 to 60 minutes inside, and leave time for your ride or walk back.

Practical example: if you are on a day trip from Busan, one guide suggests a late evening KTX from Singyeongju, so check the last train time before you plan a long evening at the pond.

FAQ

What time does Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond open and close?

VisitKorea lists operating hours of 09:00 to 22:00. A listing based on Korea Tourism Organization data gives last ticketing and admission at 21:30 and no closed days, so plan to be inside by about 21:00 to enjoy the lights.

How much is the ticket for Wolji Pond?

One listing gives 3,000 won for adults, 2,000 won for teenagers and 1,000 won for children aged 7 to 12. An older regional site shows lower prices, so confirm at the gate or by phone at +82-54-750-8655.

When is the best time to see Wolji Pond lit up?

Guides say the buildings are lit from dusk and reflect in the pond. One suggests arriving at sunset and staying until the crowds thin. In November the sun sets in the late afternoon, so an early-evening visit fits easily.

How long do I need at Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond?

A travel guide gives 30 to 60 minutes to view the site, and about an hour if you walk the whole pond edge and look at each building. Add time if you also want photos or a slow second lap.

Is Wolji Pond the same place as Anapji?

Yes. VisitKorea says the site was long called Anapji, and a piece of earthenware engraved with the name Wolji led to the original name, Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond, being restored.
