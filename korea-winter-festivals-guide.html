Winter Festivals in Korea: Dates, Costs and Tips

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 2, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Winter festivals in Korea run from late November to early February. VisitKorea lists the Hwacheon ice fishing festival for January 9 to 31, 2027, while city light shows such as Seoul's Lantern Festival and Busan's Haeundae Light Festival usually start between late November and mid-December. Confirm exact 2026 to 2027 dates with each organizer.

What should I know before I go?

Dates and fees checked November 2, 2026

Festival dates move every year, so treat any date you read, including those here, as a planning guide until the organizer confirms it. VisitKorea says its festival dates and schedules are subject to change, and its winter guide was last updated in November 2025, so it still shows the 2025 to 2026 season for most events (VisitKorea winter guide).

  • Worth knowing: The Korea Travel Helpline (1330, or +82-2-1330 from abroad) offers support in several languages including English.
  • A common mistake: Booking flights around one date. The Seoul Lantern Festival ran December 12, 2025 to January 18, 2026 in the latest official list, yet an older VisitKorea page describes it as a November event.
  • Safety note: At any frozen-river site, follow staff instructions and stay within the marked areas.
  • Local tip: Most evening displays run from 18:00 to 23:00, so dress in layers and wear shoes with grip for icy paths.

Which winter festival suits me?

Pick light festivals for easy evenings in Seoul or Busan, and ice fishing or snow festivals if you can spare a full day in Gangwon Province. VisitKorea groups the season into light festivals, New Year sunrise festivals, and ice and snow festivals.

  • Short city stay, evenings free: the Seoul Lantern Festival along Cheonggyecheon, with illuminated art and interactive experiences.
  • Busan itinerary: the Haeundae Light Festival around the beach and Gunam-ro Haeundae Square, free to enter in 2025 to 2026.
  • Active day out: the Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival, with trout ice fishing, bare-handed catches and ice sledding.
  • Sunrise on January 1: Jeongdongjin (Gangneung), Homigot (Pohang) and Hyangiram (Yeosu) held sunrise events from December 31, 2025 to January 1, 2026; Homigot draws over 2 million visitors a year, so expect heavy crowds.

Taebaek's snow festival is the fifth major option: its 33rd edition ran from January 31 to February 8, 2026 and is known for large snow sculptures with a new theme each year, so it suits travellers visiting in early February. Most Gangwon events are far from the capital, so treat them as full-day outings rather than quick evening stops, and check the return bus or train time before you settle in.

Practical example: a visitor staying in Seoul for five days in January could spend two weeknight evenings at the Cheonggyecheon lights and one full day on a Hwacheon trip, leaving the weekend for sightseeing. See our 5-day winter itinerary for a full plan.

How do I plan a festival day trip?

Confirm the dates, book transport early, and plan to arrive by mid-morning so you can see the daytime activities and the evening lights.

  1. Check the dates. Open the organizer's page and VisitKorea, or call the 1330 helpline.
  2. Choose your transport. Travel sites describe about 2 hours from Seoul to Hwacheon by express bus and about 1 hour 40 minutes to Pyeongchang's Jinbu Station by KTX, with shuttles at the site; verify schedules with the operators.
  3. Pack for the cold. Thermal layers, gloves and waterproof boots matter more than a heavy coat. Our Korea in December guide covers temperatures and clothing.
  4. Bring a payment backup. Small food stalls can be cash-friendly; see cash or card in Korea.
  5. Allow for crowds. Weekends and New Year's Eve are busiest, so aim for weekdays where possible.

FAQ

When is the Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival in 2027?

VisitKorea lists an event period of January 9 to 31, 2027, but says 2027 details will be updated later. The 2026 festival ran January 10 to February 1, so confirm the dates on the official festival site.

Are winter festivals in Korea free?

Light festivals such as the Haeundae Light Festival were free in 2025 to 2026, while ice fishing festivals charge for entry and activities. VisitKorea lists Hwacheon as paid without a price, so check the organizer.

Can I reach Korea's winter festivals without a car?

Yes for the main ones. Seoul's lantern display is on Cheonggyecheon in the city, Busan's Haeundae lights are on the beach, and Hwacheon and Pyeongchang are reachable by intercity bus or KTX plus shuttle, though journeys take about 2 hours.

What is the best winter festival for first-time visitors?

For a city trip, the Seoul Lantern Festival or Haeundae Light Festival is easiest. For a snow and ice experience, choose Hwacheon or Pyeongchang and plan a full day out of Seoul.
