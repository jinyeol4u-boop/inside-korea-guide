Korean Fried Chicken and Chimaek Explained

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 23, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

What should I know before I order Korean fried chicken?

Prices and news checked October 23, 2026

Korean fried chicken is usually sold as a whole chopped chicken, not by the piece, so it suits two people or more. The Korean fried chicken (Wikipedia) entry notes that chikin normally means chopped chicken, while tongdak is a fried whole bird that is not chopped.

  • Worth knowing: Pickled radish and a drink are the traditional accompaniments, and beer is the classic pairing.
  • A common mistake: Ordering one flavour for a group. A half-and-half box lets everyone try two styles.
  • Local tip: Some shops are set up mainly for takeaway or delivery, so check whether there are tables before you sit down.

What is chimaek and where does it come from?

Chimaek (치맥) is a slang blend of chikin (chicken) and maekju (beer), meaning fried chicken eaten with beer. The Visit Seoul Korea blog calls it a classic combination and one of the most popular choices when eating out in Korea.

According to Chimaek (Wikipedia), the pairing took its modern form in the late twentieth century, with the term first used together during the 2002 Korea–Japan World Cup, when fans watched matches on big screens in public parks. The same page says the number of chicken restaurants rose from about 10,000 to 25,000 after the tournament; treat that figure as an approximate, secondhand estimate. The Korean fried chicken (Wikipedia) page says the first modern Korean fried chicken franchise, Lims Chicken, opened in 1977 in the basement of a Seoul department store, and that yangnyeom (sweet and spicy sauced) chicken began in 1982 in a Daegu restaurant.

Practical example: if you watch a baseball game, the Visit Seoul Korea blog says you can order chicken and beer and eat it in the stands at Korean stadiums, which is a common way for visitors to try chimaek.

Which Korean fried chicken style should I order?

Choose half-and-half (banban) on your first order, then pick a favourite from the styles below. Style names below follow the Korean fried chicken (Wikipedia) article; menu names vary by chain.

How much does Korean fried chicken cost?

Major chains kept a standard chicken at around 23,000 won in October 2026, and delivery can push the bill above 30,000 won. The Korea JoongAng Daily price report (October 9, 2026) cites that chain price level and quotes a consumer critic saying some menu items cost more than 30,000 won once the delivery fee is added.

  • Delivery mark-ups: The same report notes that franchisees may charge 1,000 to 2,000 won above the suggested retail price to cover delivery app commissions.
  • Prices vary: Independent shops, flavours, boneless versions and sets can cost more or less. Treat these figures as approximate and confirm on the menu.
  • Beer is extra: Dine-in shops charge separately for beer and drinks; the menu lists each price.

For other low-cost meals, compare with our Korean street food guide.

How do I order chimaek step by step?

Pick a style, choose dine-in, pickup or delivery, then order the chicken and drinks together.

  1. Choose the style. Say or point to half-and-half (banban), or pick a single flavour from the menu.
  2. Choose how you eat. Dine-in shops typically serve chicken with pickled radish and let you order beer at the table; confirm that the shop has seating.
  3. If you order delivery, check what the app or shop needs. Delivery apps may require a Korean phone number or payment method, so read our Korean phone number guide and essential apps guide first.
  4. Order drinks with the food. Beer is the classic pairing, and carbonated soft drinks are the other common choice.
  5. Pay and tidy up. Follow the shop's system, and see how to order at a Korean restaurant for call buttons, kiosks and side dishes.

Where can visitors enjoy chimaek?

Try a chicken restaurant, a baseball stadium or, in early summer, a chicken and beer festival.

  • Chicken restaurants: Evening meals with colleagues or friends are the usual setting, and chimaek is especially popular in summer according to the Visit Seoul Korea blog.
  • Baseball stadiums: The same blog says you can order chicken and beer and eat in the stands.
  • Daegu CHIMAC Festival: The Korea Times report on the 2026 Daegu CHIMAC Festival says the 2026 festival ran July 1 to 5 at Duryu Park in Daegu, with a Global Lounge for foreign visitors. Dates change each year, so check the official announcement before planning.
  • Han River parks: Picnics with delivered food are a well-known local habit, but alcohol rules vary. A Han River-wide ban was discussed and reported on hold by the Korea Herald (January 5, 2024); local no-drinking zones with fines of up to 100,000 won exist in some districts. Check park signs and Seoul city notices before you drink outdoors.

FAQ

What does chimaek mean?

Chimaek (치맥) combines chikin (chicken) and maekju (beer). It means eating Korean fried chicken with beer, usually in the evening.

Which Korean fried chicken should a first-timer order?

Order half-and-half (banban), which gives one half plain huraideu and one half sweet-spicy yangnyeom, so you can compare both styles in one box.

How much is Korean fried chicken?

Major chains kept their standard chicken at around 23,000 won in October 2026, and delivery fees or app mark-ups can push the total above 30,000 won.

Can I drink beer at the Han River parks?

Rules vary by place and can change. A Han River-wide alcohol ban was discussed but reported on hold in January 2024, so check signs at the park and the city's official notices.
