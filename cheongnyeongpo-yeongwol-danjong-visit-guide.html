Cheongnyeongpo, Yeongwol: Danjong's Exile Site Guide

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 28, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Cheongnyeongpo costs 3,000 won for adults, ferry included, and opens 09:00 to 18:00 with last entry at 17:00, usually closed Mondays. It is where the boy king Danjong was exiled in 1457, the story behind The King's Warden. Pair it with his tomb, Jangneung.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 28, 2026

Cheongnyeongpo is reached only by a short boat ride, closes on most Mondays and stops admitting visitors at 17:00. Plan to arrive by mid-afternoon, especially in late autumn and winter.

  • Worth knowing: The entry fee includes the ferry, and high water or bad weather can stop the boat during opening hours. The ticket office number listed is 033-372-1240.
  • A common mistake: Expecting to see the film village. The movie is set here, but parts were shot elsewhere, including the Mungyeong Saejae set.
  • Winter note: I found no published rule for river ice. Phone ahead (or ask a Korean speaker to) after a hard freeze.

What is The King's Warden about?

The King's Warden (왕과 사는 남자) is a 2026 historical drama about the deposed boy king Danjong and the village chief who looks after him during his exile in Yeongwol. It was directed by Jang Hang-jun and released on February 4, 2026, with Yoo Hae-jin as village chief Eom Heung-do and Park Ji-hoon as Danjong.

  • Box office: It passed 16 million admissions on April 5, 2026, which The Korea Times reported as third on Korea's all-time chart at that point.
  • Awards: At the Blue Dragon Film Awards on November 25, 2026, the film won the audience award for most popular film, and Park Ji-hoon won Best New Actor and a Popular Star award. See our Blue Dragon guide for the night itself.
  • Watching it now: I could not verify which streaming services carry it in each country, so search your local platforms for the title.
  • Fact and fiction: It is a dramatisation. Treat dialogue and friendships on screen as storytelling, not as the historical record.

Who was King Danjong?

Danjong was Joseon's sixth king, who took the throne as a child in 1452, was forced to give it up to his uncle in 1455 and died in exile in Yeongwol in 1457. His uncle became King Sejo.

How do I visit Cheongnyeongpo?

Buy a ticket at the riverside entrance (3,000 won for adults), take the boat across and walk the forested peninsula; it opens 09:00 to 18:00. The address is 133 Cheongnyeongpo-ro, Yeongwol, Gangwon State.

The river bends around three sides of the land, with a steep ridge behind it, which is why it worked as a place of confinement. The Korea Heritage Service lists it as a Scenic Site, designated December 26, 2008.

  • Exile residence: A reconstruction; the original was lost to flooding.
  • Gwaneumsong pine: A very old pine, protected as a Natural Monument, linked in local tradition to Danjong.
  • Manghyangtap and Nosandae: A small stone tower and a lookout associated with the young king looking toward home.
  • Etiquette: Stay on the paths, do not touch or climb the pine or its fence, and keep voices low around the memorial markers.

What can I see at Jangneung, Danjong's tomb?

Jangneung is Danjong's royal tomb in Yeongwol town, part of the UNESCO-listed Royal Tombs of the Joseon Dynasty, with adult admission of 2,000 won. It sits far from the cluster of royal tombs around Seoul.

The address is 190 Danjong-ro, Yeongwol-eup. The Royal Tombs and Palaces service lists 2,000 won for adults, 1,500 won for middle and high school students and 1,000 won for children, and notes that Yeongwol County manages this tomb separately. Travel guides report Monday closures, plus Seollal and Chuseok days, and last entry around 17:30; I could not confirm those on an official page. A history hall about Danjong is also on the grounds. Allow 60 to 90 minutes.

How do I get to Yeongwol from Seoul?

Take a train from Cheongnyangni Station to Yeongwol Station, about 2 to 2.5 hours, then a short taxi ride. Trains are few, so check the return time before you leave.

  • Trains: Yeongwol is on the Taebaek Line. A third-party timetable for the return direction showed five daily Mugunghwa and ITX-Maum trains, about 1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, with a 16,000 won fare. Confirm both directions on Korail.
  • Taxi: A Korean travel blog estimates 7 to 10 minutes and about 5,000 to 6,000 won from Yeongwol Station to Cheongnyeongpo.
  • Local bus: Buses run but are infrequent; do not rely on them without checking times on the day.
  • Naming it: Show drivers 청령포 (Cheongnyeongpo) or 장릉 (Jangneung). Our Naver Map guide helps with routes.

How do I plan a one-day Danjong trip?

Catch a morning train, see Cheongnyeongpo first, then Jangneung, and return on an evening train.

  1. Pick a day: Choose Tuesday to Sunday and check the weather and river conditions.
  2. Book both trains: Reserve the outward and return seats together; see our rail booking guide for how foreigners buy Korail tickets.
  3. Taxi to Cheongnyeongpo: Cross by boat and allow about an hour or more.
  4. Lunch in town: Eat in Yeongwol-eup between the two sites.
  5. Jangneung: Visit the tomb and history hall, then return to the station with time to spare.

Practical example: a visitor who arrives late morning can see Cheongnyeongpo before lunch and Jangneung after it, leaving a buffer for an evening train. In winter, dress for riverside cold using our winter packing guide.

FAQ

Is Cheongnyeongpo where The King's Warden was filmed?

The film is set at Cheongnyeongpo, but it was shot partly elsewhere, including the Mungyeong Saejae movie set. Visit Yeongwol for the history behind the story rather than for recognisable sets.

How much does it cost to visit Cheongnyeongpo?

The listed adult fee is 3,000 won and includes the boat crossing. Youth pay 2,500 won, children 2,000 won and visitors 65 or older 1,000 won. Confirm on the day.

Is Cheongnyeongpo open on Mondays?

It is normally closed on Mondays and on Seollal and Chuseok day. When a Monday is a public or substitute holiday, the closing day may move to a following weekday.

Can I do Yeongwol as a day trip from Seoul?

Yes, if you plan around the few trains. The ride from Cheongnyangni takes roughly 2 to 2.5 hours each way, which leaves time for Cheongnyeongpo and Jangneung.

Where is King Danjong buried?

Danjong is buried at Jangneung in Yeongwol-eup. It is one of the Joseon royal tombs listed by UNESCO in 2009, with an adult fee of 2,000 won.
