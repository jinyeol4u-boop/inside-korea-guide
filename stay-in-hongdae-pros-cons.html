Staying in Hongdae: Pros, Cons and Who It Suits

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Hongdae suits you if you want live music, cafes and late nights, and AREX all-stop trains reach Hongik Univ. Station directly (Seoul Station fare: 4,150 to 4,750 won, just under 60 minutes). Expect crowds and noise, so light sleepers should request quiet side-street rooms or consider other areas.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked October 22, 2026

Hongdae is the lively area around Hongik University in Mapo-gu, and its busiest streets sit around two different stations. VisitKorea gives the address as 20 Hongik-ro, Mapo-gu, and describes a youthful neighborhood with shops, unique cafes and restaurants, and streets filled with buskers and dance performances.

  • Worth knowing: Visit Seoul names Hongik Univ. Station on Line 2 as the main entry point. Several sights listed by Visit Seoul, such as the mural street and the Saturday free market, are measured from Sangsu Station (Line 6) Exit 2 instead.
  • A common mistake: Booking a place near Sangsu Station and assuming it is a short walk to the AREX platform. Check the walk from your hotel to Hongik Univ. Station with luggage before you pay.
  • Local tip: The Mapo Tourist Information Center answers visitor questions at 02-334-7878, a number listed on Visit Seoul's Hongdae Mural Street page.

Is Hongdae a good place to stay in Seoul?

Yes, if evening energy and a direct airport train matter more to you than quiet. The official pages describe a district built around street culture, which is a draw for some travelers and a drawback for others.

Pros

  • A direct airport train. Visit Seoul says Hongik Univ. Station is served by the AREX all-stop train and is a transfer station with Subway Line 2 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line.
  • Street performances. Visit Seoul's editors describe singing, dance and instrumental busking on the pedestrian street, with weekday shows in the evening and weekend shows from noon (the page warns its busking details may be dated).
  • Free things to see. Hongdae Mural Street is always open and free, and about 545 m from Sangsu Station Exit 2. The Hongdae Free Market runs Saturdays from 13:00 to 18:00, March to November, and sells original creative work.
  • Cafes and indie venues. The same editors' page lists Indiespace, an indie film theater near Exit 8, and a two-floor cafe that stays open until 23:00.

Cons

  • Crowds and noise. Visit Seoul describes Hongdae as crowded, with bright signboards and street music. Another Visit Seoul page lists Club nb as open 19:00 to 05:30 about 350 m from Sangsu Exit 2, so late-night noise is part of the area (confirm current hours).
  • Far from the palaces. Hongdae is in western Seoul, so Gyeongbokgung and Insadong need a subway ride (our judgment; check travel times in your map app).
  • Less variety in some directions. The official descriptions focus on youth culture, so travelers who prefer a residential or traditional feel may be happier elsewhere (our judgment).

Practical example: a four-night trip with a late-evening arrival works well here, because the all-stop train runs to Hongik Univ. Station and the busking street and cafes are close to the exits.

How do I get to Hongdae from Incheon Airport?

Take the AREX all-stop train to Hongik Univ. Station; the express train skips it. VisitKorea's airport guide lists the all-stop train at about 59 minutes to Seoul Station from Terminal 1 and about 66 minutes from Terminal 2, and names Hongik University as one of its stops. Hongik Univ. comes before Seoul Station on the line, but no source we read gives an exact time for it.

Who should stay in Hongdae, and who should not?

Choose Hongdae for music, cafes and late evenings; choose Myeongdong, Insadong or Gwanghwamun for shopping or history. Our Myeongdong vs Hongdae vs Insadong guide compares the three areas by day.

How do I choose a place to stay in Hongdae?

Pick the station first, then the street, and ask about noise before you book. Hongik Univ. Station is the practical anchor because it serves the airport train and Line 2.

  1. Choose your station. Stay within a short walk of Hongik Univ. Station for the AREX, or near Sangsu Station (Line 6) for the mural street and free market.
  2. Plan the airport trip. Confirm the AREX all-stop route, and note the late-night bus N6002 if your flight lands after 23:30.
  3. Ask about noise. Request an upper-floor room away from the main pedestrian street and clubs.
  4. Check the rules. Confirm the cancellation policy before you pay, and read current guest reviews on the booking site.

FAQ

Is Hongdae a good area to stay in Seoul for first-timers?

Yes, if you want street music, cafes and evenings out. Hongik Univ. Station links Line 2, the Gyeongui-Jungang Line and the AREX airport train. Choose another area if you need quiet nights or want to stay near the palaces.

How do I get from Incheon Airport to Hongdae?

Take the AREX all-stop train to Hongik Univ. Station. The express train does not stop there. Visit Seoul lists the all-stop fare to Seoul Station as 4,150 to 4,750 won; confirm the Hongik fare with AREX.

Is there a late-night airport bus to Hongdae?

Yes. airport.kr lists route N6002 from Terminal 1 with a Hongdae stop about 45 minutes after departure, for 17,000 won (adult). Departures run between 00:00 and 04:40, so verify the timetable first.

Is Hongdae noisy at night?

Often. Visit Seoul describes the area as crowded, with bright signboards and street music, and lists Club nb as open 19:00 to 05:30. Ask for an upper-floor room away from the main streets.
