Bulguksa and Seokguram: How to Visit Both in Half a Day

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 7, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Bulguksa and Seokguram are both UNESCO-listed, about 9 km apart by road, and fit into four to five hours. VisitKorea lists both as free, open 09:00 to 18:00 with exit by 19:00. See Bulguksa first, ride Bus 12 uphill (about 30 minutes, as reported), then return by bus or taxi.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 7, 2026

Plan Bulguksa first and Seokguram second, start before midday, and treat the bus times as something to confirm on the day. The two sites are on the same mountain but are separate stops, and public transport between them is the weak link in the plan.

  • Worth knowing: Hours and fees change. VisitKorea lists Bulguksa and Seokguram as free and open all year, 09:00-18:00 with exit time 19:00, but notes that hours may change.
  • A common mistake: Leaving Seokguram until late. A guide checked for this article reports the last bus down from Seokguram around 18:00 in winter, so arriving at the top late risks a taxi ride.
  • Local tip: Wear shoes with grip. The route between the sites is a hiking trail of about 3 km, and the grotto approach is a short uphill walk from the bus stop.

Why visit Bulguksa and Seokguram together?

They were listed together as one UNESCO World Heritage property, and each makes more sense when you have seen the other. The UNESCO World Heritage Centre describes Bulguksa as a wooden temple complex on raised stone terraces, designed to represent the Buddhist land of paradise, and Seokguram as a granite grotto holding a monumental Buddha facing the sea. UNESCO dates Bulguksa to 774 and Seokguram to the 8th century.

Practical example: at Bulguksa you walk through the courtyard and stone terraces in the valley, then at Seokguram you see the Buddha chamber on the ridge above, so the same Silla-era builders' work appears at two different scales in a single morning.

How do I get from Gyeongju to Bulguksa?

City Bus 10 or 11 from the Gyeongju bus terminal or station area is the usual public route, and Bus 700 is reported from Singyeongju KTX Station. Bus numbers come from guides rather than the official pages, so check the live route in a map app.

  • From Seoul by train: KTX from Seoul Station to Singyeongju Station takes about 2 hours 10 minutes, according to Visit Korea Daily. See our guide to booking KTX tickets.
  • From Singyeongju Station: Bus 700 to Bulguksa is reported at about 30 minutes.
  • From central Gyeongju: Bus 10 or 11 is reported at about 40 minutes; the bus stop is a 3 to 5 minute walk from the entrance, per windsubok8.kr.
  • By taxi or car: Bulguksa has parking, and fees vary by vehicle size and season according to the same guide.

How do I get from Bulguksa to Seokguram?

Take Bus 12 uphill, which is reported at about 30 minutes, or a taxi; the sites are about 3 km apart on a hiking trail and about 9 km by road. VisitKorea gives the distances, and the same page notes parking at Seokguram.

What does a realistic half-day schedule look like?

Start at Bulguksa by about 09:00, leave for Seokguram by 11:00, and aim to be heading down by early afternoon. This sample assumes the times reported above and leaves buffer for waiting.

  1. Reach Bulguksa at opening. Opening is 09:00 per VisitKorea; arriving then also avoids tour groups.
  2. Spend 1 to 1.5 hours inside. A guide checked for this article suggests 45 minutes to 1 hour for highlights, or 1 to 1.5 hours with photos.
  3. Take Bus 12 or a taxi uphill. Check the next departure at the stop before you rely on it.
  4. Visit Seokguram for 45 to 60 minutes. Budget for the uphill walk from the stop and any queue.
  5. Head back down. Check the last bus time before you go in, then return to Gyeongju by Bus 10 or 11.

Practical example: if the next Bus 12 is more than 30 minutes away, splitting a taxi (about 8,000 to 10,000 won as reported) between two or three people can be the better use of daylight.

FAQ

Is it possible to see Bulguksa and Seokguram in one morning?

Yes, if you start early. Allow about 1 to 1.5 hours at Bulguksa, 20 to 30 minutes each way between the sites and 45 to 60 minutes at Seokguram, which makes roughly four to five hours including waiting for buses.

Do Bulguksa and Seokguram charge admission?

VisitKorea currently lists both as free, with the exit time at 19:00. Older guides quote 6,000 won for adults, so check the temple's official page or call before you go.

How do I get from Bulguksa to Seokguram?

Take the local Bus 12 uphill, which guides report takes about 30 minutes, or a taxi, reported at roughly 8,000 to 10,000 won. The sites are about 3 km apart on a hiking trail and about 9 km by road.

Which bus goes from Gyeongju to Bulguksa?

Guides report City Bus 10 or 11 from the Gyeongju bus terminal or station area, and Bus 700 from Singyeongju KTX Station. Confirm the route in Naver Map or Kakao Map on the day.
