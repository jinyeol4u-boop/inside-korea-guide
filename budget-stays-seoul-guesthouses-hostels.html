Budget Accommodation in Seoul: Guesthouses and Hostels

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Hostel dorm beds start at about 25,000 won a night, according to one unofficial 2026 guide, while VisitKorea calls guesthouses relatively inexpensive and motels cost-effective. For airport-train access, base yourself near Hongik University or Seoul Station, and confirm registration, bedding and cancellation terms before you pay.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked October 12, 2026

Official tourism pages describe budget stays by type but publish no nightly prices, so treat any price you see as a guide and confirm it on the booking page. VisitKorea says guesthouses are relatively inexpensive and motels are the ultimate cost-effective accommodation. That page was last updated on October 17, 2025, so details may have changed.

  • Worth knowing: VisitKorea warns that some motels have the word "hotel" in their names, so the name alone does not tell you what kind of property it is.
  • A common mistake: Booking a dorm bed without checking the room type. VisitKorea lists dormitories, one- to two-person rooms and female-only guesthouses as separate options.
  • Local tip: Take your shoes off at the door. Visit Seoul says this applies when entering any residence, temple or guesthouse.

What types of budget stays are there in Seoul?

The main budget choices are guesthouses and hostels, motels, and district-registered Seoul Stay lodging, and each suits a different traveller. The table summarises what the official pages say. Prices are not given there, so the last column is an unofficial estimate.

Practical example: a solo traveller who wants to meet people can pick a dorm in a guesthouse that hosts social events, while two friends who want privacy can compare a private guesthouse room with a motel room at similar rates.

How much does a budget stay cost?

One unofficial guide puts a hostel dorm bed at about 25,000 to 45,000 won a night in 2026, but official pages list no rates. The Seoul Tourism Guide is a private site that says it earns commission through affiliate links, so use its figures only to plan a rough budget.

  • Weekday versus weekend: That guide says Friday and Saturday rates run 20 to 30% higher, so a Sunday to Thursday stay may cost less.
  • Longer stays: It also says some properties discount stays of seven or more nights, so ask before booking.
  • Cash: It notes that small guesthouses and motels may not take online bookings, so carry some cash.

Practical example: five weeknights in a dorm at the unofficial range works out to roughly 125,000 to 225,000 won in total (5 x 25,000 to 5 x 45,000). Add a weekend night and the total may rise, so check the exact nightly rates before you pay.

Which area suits a budget stay?

Hongik University and Seoul Station are the practical starting points because both are AREX all-stop stops from Incheon Airport. The Visit Seoul Getting to Seoul page lists the all-stop stops, including Digital Media City, Hongik University and Seoul Station.

  • Hongik University (Hongdae): A transfer station for Subway Line 2 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, according to Visit Seoul, which makes it easy to reach other districts.
  • Seoul Station: The last AREX stop and a transfer for Subway Lines 1 and 4, KTX and the Gyeongui Line, so it suits early departures and day trips.
  • Airport train details: All-stop trains run every 5 to 10 minutes and take just under 60 minutes to Seoul Station, with fares of 4,150 to 4,750 won, per Visit Seoul. Airport buses cost 5,000 to 15,000 won and take about 60 to 80 minutes.

The Visit Seoul accommodation page also lets you browse registered stays by region, including Gwanghwamun, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun, Hongdae, Yeouido, Itaewon, Gangnam and Jamsil. How do I check a stay is registered?

Look for a Seoul Stay plaque or listing, because Visit Seoul says registered properties display an official government plaque. According to the Seoul Stay guide, Seoul Stay means accommodation registered with its local district, with support from the Seoul Metropolitan Government.

  • Best Seoul Stay: The city makes an annual selection of urban and hanok stays with excellent facilities and services, and winners have been listed since 2021.
  • Browse and sort: The Visit Seoul accommodation page describes Seoul Stay as urban guest houses and hanok guesthouses operating legally in Seoul, and its listings can be sorted by Best Seoul Stay.
  • Read each listing: Properties differ. Examples on that page show guest limits, such as a two-bedroom unit for up to four guests and another with a minimum of two and a maximum of six, and individual cancellation conditions, so read each one.
  • Need help: The 1330 Korea Travel Helpline is +82-2-1330, according to VisitKorea, and Seoul Stay lists 1330 for general tourism inquiries.

How do I book a budget stay step by step?

Choose the type and area first, then compare rooms, check the rules and confirm cancellation terms before paying.

  1. Pick a type. Decide between a dorm bed, a private guesthouse room, a motel or a Seoul Stay home.
  2. Pick an area. Choose Hongik University or Seoul Station if you want a direct airport train, or browse other regions on Visit Seoul.
  3. Check the room. Confirm the room type, guest limit, bedding and bathroom. Ask for female-only dorms if you want one.
  4. Check the name. If a listing says "hotel", look at the room photos and description, since some motels use that word.
  5. Read the cancellation terms. Terms vary by property, so check them before paying.
  6. Plan your arrival. Tell the host your arrival time, especially for small properties, and carry some cash in case a motel or guesthouse does not accept online payment.

FAQ

Are hostels and guesthouses cheaper than hotels in Seoul?

VisitKorea describes guesthouses as relatively inexpensive compared with other accommodation types and motels as the ultimate cost-effective option. It gives no prices, so compare current nightly rates on the booking page you use.

What is the difference between a guesthouse and a motel in Korea?

VisitKorea says guesthouses offer dormitories, one- to two-person rooms and sometimes female-only rooms, and some host social events. Motels are cost-effective rooms, often downtown, and some include the word "hotel" in their name.

What is Seoul Stay?

Seoul Stay refers to accommodation registered with its local district, in two categories: urban B&B for foreign tourists and hanok experience lodging. Registered properties display an official plaque, according to Visit Seoul.

Do I take my shoes off in a guesthouse?

Yes. Visit Seoul's etiquette page says to take your shoes off at the door when entering any residence, temple or guesthouse.

Which area is best for a budget stay near the airport train?

Hongik University Station and Seoul Station are both AREX all-stop stops, according to Visit Seoul. Hongik University is also a transfer station for Subway Line 2 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, so it is a practical base for many first-timers.
