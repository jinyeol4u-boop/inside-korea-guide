Mungyeong Saejae Open Set: How to Visit from Seoul

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 27, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Allow about 93 minutes on KTX-Eum from Pangyo to Mungyeong, then a short local bus ride to Mungyeong Saejae Provincial Park. The park is free; its drama open set costs 2,000 won for adults and, in winter, is reported to open 09:00 to 17:00.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 27, 2026

Plan around the train timetable, because only a few KTX-Eum services run each day and the last convenient buses leave the park in the early evening. Winter daylight and a 17:00 set closing also make a morning start the sensible choice.

  • Worth knowing: The park and the drama set are two different things. VisitKorea lists the park as free and open all day, while the set inside it charges admission and keeps its own hours.
  • A common mistake: Treating Mungyeong Station as the park entrance. You still need a bus or taxi, and Jeomchon, Mungyeong-eup and Saejae are separate places, as a November 2026 travel report points out.
  • Local tip: Show the driver or search your map app with the Korean name 문경새재도립공원.

What is the Mungyeong Saejae Open Set?

It is an outdoor period set of palace halls, tile-roofed houses and thatched cottages built for historical dramas, set among the mountains of Mungyeong Saejae. It is a place to walk through, not an indoor studio tour.

  • Origin: According to the Gyeongbukzine guide (a third-party site), KBS opened it in February 2000 behind the first gate for Goryeo-era dramas, and it now counts 97 buildings.
  • Dramas often associated with it: The same guide lists Taejo Wang Geon, Dae Jo Yeong, The Slave Hunters (Chuno) and The Moon Embracing the Sun among others. We could not read an official production list, so treat it as indicative.
  • Current drama: KBS 2TV's historical epic Munmu, which premiered on November 14, 2026, filmed major scenes at the Mungyeong Saejae set, the Gaeun open set and Gomosanseong Fortress, iNews24 reported on November 16. See our Munmu guide for the story and broadcast details.
  • What to expect: Sets are working backdrops. Interiors may be closed or empty, and an area can look different from what you saw on screen.

Practical example: if you watched an episode of Munmu last weekend, walk the set looking for gates, courtyards and walls rather than one exact shot. No official scene-by-scene map was found.

How do I get there from Seoul?

Take the KTX-Eum from Pangyo Station to Mungyeong Station, then a city bus or taxi to the park. Pangyo is just south of Seoul and is reached from Gangnam on the Shinbundang subway line.

  • Station to park: The bus company's train connection table shows buses from Mungyeong Station toward Saejae at 10:10 (express) and 14:15 (express), among other times, which fit the two earlier arrivals. News and blog accounts put the ride at roughly 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Bus fare: Mungyeong announced that city buses would be free for everyone, visitors included, from January 1, 2025.
  • Taxi: A 2025 travel blog paid about 12,000 won from the station to the park.
  • Going back: The company table lists Saejae-to-station buses at 14:10 and 18:25, but other accounts give a slightly earlier last departure. Read the timetable posted at the stop when you arrive.

How much does it cost and when is it open?

The park and its museums are listed as free, and the open set costs 2,000 won for adults. The electric shuttle is a separate, optional fare.

How far should I walk on the old pass?

For a first visit, walk to the first gate and the open set; add the second gate only if you have time, because the full three-gate route takes about four hours round trip.

Saejae, also called Joryeong, was a mountain crossing on the old road between the capital and the southeast. Its three gates, Juheulgwan, Jogokgwan and Joryeonggwan, are protected historic remains, according to VisitKorea. The November 2026 report gives these distances:

  • Gate 1 (Juheulgwan) to Gate 2 (Jogokgwan): about 3 km.
  • Gate 2 to Gate 3 (Joryeonggwan): about 3.5 km.
  • All three: about 6.5 km one way, climbing as you go, so about 13 km there and back, plus the walk from the parking area to Gate 1.

Practical example: with the 09:56 arrival and a 14:36 return train, see the open set and Gate 1 only. If you stay for the 18:55 train, you have room to walk to Gate 2 and back before the light fades.

How do I plan the day?

Take the 08:22 from Pangyo, see the set first, walk part of the pass after it and return on the 18:55.

  1. The day before: Book both KTX-Eum seats and check the forecast for the mountains.
  2. 08:22: Leave Pangyo and arrive at Mungyeong at 09:56.
  3. About 10:10: Ride the connecting bus toward Saejae, or take a taxi.
  4. Late morning: Visit the Old Road Museum, then the open set.
  5. Midday: Have lunch or a packed snack, then walk through Gate 1 toward Gate 2 as far as your pace allows.
  6. Mid-afternoon: Turn back early enough to reach the bus stop well before the last bus.
  7. 18:55: Ride back to Pangyo, arriving 20:26.

What etiquette and safety rules matter?

Respect filming areas and protected structures, and dress for a mountain valley rather than a city street.

  • Filming: If a crew is working, follow staff directions, stay behind barriers and do not photograph performers or equipment when asked not to.
  • Heritage: Do not climb on gates, walls, roofs or props.
  • Winter: Wear shoes with grip and layers. Shaded stretches can be icy, and the trail is poorly lit after dark.
  • Supplies: Carry water and some cash, especially if you plan to walk beyond Gate 1.

FAQ

Is Mungyeong Saejae a day trip from Seoul?

Yes. KTX-Eum runs from Pangyo to Mungyeong in about 1 hour 33 minutes, with four round trips a day in the timetable data we checked. A morning train out and an evening train back leave several hours for the open set and part of the old pass.

How much is the Mungyeong Saejae Open Set?

VisitKorea lists 2,000 won for adults, 1,000 won for teenagers and 500 won for children. The provincial park itself and the Old Road Museum are listed as free.

What time does the open set close?

A November 2026 report gives winter hours of 09:00 to 17:00 from November to February. A third-party guide lists 09:00 to 18:00 from March to October. Confirm on the day, especially around holidays.

Do I have to hike to see the drama set?

No. The set sits inside the park near the first gate, so you can visit it without walking the whole pass. The full route past all three gates is a much longer walk of about four hours round trip.
