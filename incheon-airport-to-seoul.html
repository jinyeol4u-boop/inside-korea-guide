Incheon Airport to Seoul: AREX vs Bus vs Taxi

By Dragon Kim · Updated September 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

For most travellers the AREX all-stop train is best: about 4,150 to 4,750 won and roughly 59 minutes to Seoul Station. Take the 43-minute AREX Express for a reserved seat, an airport limousine bus (about 17,000 won) if it stops near your hotel, and a taxi (65,000 won and up) for groups or late nights.

What should I know before I arrive?

Fares and times checked September 30, 2026

Decide before you land, because the airport splits into three separate exits. The AREX station is in the basement of Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, the bus stops are outside the arrivals hall, and the official taxi stands are marked by numbered zones.

  • Worth knowing: Express and all-stop trains use different platforms, so check the board before you go down.
  • A common mistake: Accepting a ride from a driver who approaches you in the arrivals hall. Use the marked taxi stands instead.
  • Local tip: Load a T-money card before you leave the airport. It works on the AREX, subway and city buses.

Which option is best from Incheon Airport?

The AREX all-stop train is the best default; choose the bus or taxi only when your situation calls for it. The Airport Railroad (AREX) avoids road traffic, runs every few minutes and ends at Seoul Station, where Subway Lines 1 and 4 continue into the city.

  • Travelling light, going to Seoul Station, Hongdae or Gongdeok: AREX.
  • Hotel near a limousine bus stop, with big suitcases: bus, so you avoid stairs and transfers.
  • Three or more people, or arriving after the trains stop: taxi.

Practical example: a solo traveller staying in Hongdae can take the AREX all-stop train straight to Hongik Univ. Station without changing lines.

How much does each option cost?

Roughly 4,150 won by train, about 17,000 won by bus and 65,000 won or more by taxi. Figures are approximate and change; always confirm on the official pages listed below.

Late-night taxi trips (22:00 to 04:00) add a surcharge, and the Incheon Airport taxi guide warns against unlicensed drivers, so use the marked taxi stands.

How do I ride the AREX to Seoul?

Follow the AREX signs to the basement level, tap a T-money card or buy a ticket, and board an all-stop train toward Seoul Station.

  1. Follow the signs. After customs, look for "Airport Railroad" and go down to the platforms.
  2. Pay. Tap a T-money card, or buy a single-journey ticket at a machine. Express tickets are sold at a separate counter and online.
  3. Choose the train. Express and all-stop trains use different platforms, so check the board before you go down.
  4. Ride and transfer. Get off at Seoul Station, Digital Media City, Hongik Univ. or Gongdeok, or change to Subway Line 1 or 4 at Seoul Station.

FAQ

What is the cheapest way from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

The AREX all-stop train, at roughly 4,150 to 4,750 won to Seoul Station with a T-money card.

Is the AREX Express worth the extra cost?

Only if you want a reserved seat and the fastest 43-minute ride to Seoul Station; the all-stop train is about 16 minutes slower and far cheaper.

Should I take a taxi from Incheon Airport?

A taxi suits groups of three or more, heavy luggage or late-night arrivals. Expect roughly 65,000 to 80,000 won plus tolls, and use the official taxi stands.
