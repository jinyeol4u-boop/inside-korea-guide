Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Inside Korea Guide ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.
  • Stories you submit to Korea Voices: if you share a story on our Korea Voices page, we store the name or nickname (optional), country, headline, story, topic and visit type you enter, and publish them after review. We do not ask for your email address. To limit spam, we also store a one-way, non-reversible code derived from your IP address; we cannot use it to identify you. To have a published story removed, contact us through the Contact page with its headline.
  • Automatically collected information: like most websites, this site and its service providers automatically receive standard log data such as your browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. This site uses cookies to understand how visitors use it and to show advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not work as expected.

3. Advertising: Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this site. It collects information such as pages visited and time spent on the site, in aggregated form. You can prevent Google Analytics from using your data by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Hosting

This site is served by a third-party web hosting provider, which may process standard server log data (such as IP address and request time) to deliver and secure the site.

6. Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to booking and travel services. If you click one, the destination site may place its own cookie to record that you came from our site. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads and non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time. You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing.

8. Visitors in California and other US states

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Contact us using the details below to make a request.
