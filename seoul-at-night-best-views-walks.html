Seoul at Night: The Best Night Views and Evening Walks

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

The best free night view in Seoul is Naksan Park, open 24 hours along the old city wall. For a tower view, N Seoul Tower's observatory costs 29,000 won and closes at 22:30 on weekdays. The Banpo Bridge fountain shows run April to October, with evening shows from 19:30.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked October 21, 2026

Seoul's night views fall into three types: free hill and city-wall viewpoints, ticketed towers and observatories, and seasonal shows on the Han River. Visit Seoul describes Naksan Park as famous for the beauty of its nighttime views and the city wall, while Seoul Metropolitan Government calls its nightscape a must-see in person.

  • Worth knowing: The Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain only operates from April to October, so as of October 21, 2026 the season is nearly over. It does not run in November to March.
  • A common mistake: Arriving at a tower just before closing. Visit Seoul says the last entry to N Seoul Tower is 30 minutes before closing time.
  • Local tip: Pick the venue by what you want to look at. A skyline from a hillside is free, while a high observatory costs money, and weather can change fountain times, so confirm the day before.

Which Seoul night view spots are worth your time?

For most first-time visitors, Naksan Park (free) and N Seoul Tower (paid) give the widest city views, while Banpo Bridge adds a water-and-light show. The table compares the spots listed by Visit Seoul; prices and hours can change, so confirm before you go.

How do I walk Naksan Park and the city wall at night?

Take Line 4 to Hyehwa Station, leave by Exit 2, and walk about 627 m to Naksan Park, which is free and open around the clock. The park sits on the Seoul City Wall (Hanyangdoseong), which Seoul Metropolitan Government says is about 18.6 km long.

  1. Get there. Hyehwa Station (Line 4) Exit 2 is the closest station listed by Visit Seoul.
  2. Climb into the park. The address is 41 Naksan-gil, Jongno-gu. The park is known for panoramic views of downtown Seoul.
  3. Follow the wall. Seoul Metropolitan Government describes an excursion trail from Hyehwa to Dongdaemun along the city wall, with a well-lit path at night.
  4. Add Ihwa Village if you have time. The same page mentions colorful murals in nearby Ihwa Village, best seen before dark.
  5. Leave by a lit route. Keep to lit paths and walk back to a station when you are done.

For another free evening route, see our Cheonggyecheon to Gwanghwamun walk and the wider list of free things to do in Seoul.

How do I see N Seoul Tower and its view after dark?

Visit Seoul lists the tower as open daily, 10:00 to 22:30 on weekdays and 10:00 to 23:00 on weekends, with the observatory at 29,000 won for adults and 23,000 won for children. The tower sits at the summit of Namsan Mountain, about 480 m above sea level, and Visit Seoul says digital art is projected onto the tower at night.

  • Cable car: Runs from 10:00 to 23:00 and costs 15,000 won round trip or 12,000 won one way for adults, per Visit Seoul.
  • Bus: From Chungmuro Station (Lines 3 and 4) Exit 2, take Bus 01 (Namsan Circular) to the Namsan Seoul Tower stop, or from Dongdaemun Station (Line 3) Exit 6.
  • Timing: The last entry is 30 minutes before closing, so allow time for queues.
  • Light show times: The listing gives no schedule for the tower lighting, so check before you go.

How do I watch the Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain?

The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain operates every day from April to October, with evening shows from 19:30 and each show lasting 20 minutes. VisitKorea says 200 lights illuminate the fountain after sunset, and that show times are subject to change with the weather.

Banpo Bridge fountain show times, checked October 21, 2026 (confirm before you go)

  1. Take the subway. Visit Seoul lists Express Bus Terminal Station (Lines 3, 7 and 9) Exit 8-1; the distance is given as 1.1 km on one Visit Seoul page and 850 m on another, so allow roughly 15 to 20 minutes on foot.
  2. Arrive 20 to 30 minutes early. The bridge is in Seocho-gu (Banpo-dong), and a visitor note on Visit Seoul warns of heavy traffic, so walking from the station can be faster than a ride-hail.
  3. Choose a viewpoint. VisitKorea mentions views from the hills, the Hangang River observation deck and the moon-shaped Moonlight Square.
  4. Check the weather. Shows can change with conditions, so look at the day's forecast.

How can I fit it into one evening?

Pair a free city-wall walk with one ticketed or scheduled highlight, because the venues sit on opposite sides of the city. The sample plan below is built only from the official hours above, so adjust it to the season.

  1. Late afternoon: Ride Line 4 to Hyehwa Station and walk Naksan Park and the city wall before the lights come on.
  2. Early evening: Return to the subway and head to Express Bus Terminal Station for the 19:30 or 20:00 fountain show (April to October only).
  3. Alternative after dark: From November to March, replace the fountain with N Seoul Tower, entering before 22:00 on weekdays or 22:30 on weekends (last entry is 30 minutes before closing).

Practical example: on a Friday in September, a visitor could finish Naksan Park by about 18:30, travel across the city, and aim for the 20:30 or 21:00 fountain shows rather than the earlier ones, which leaves a buffer for the subway ride.

FAQ

When is the best time to see Seoul's night views?

Go after dark on a clear evening. N Seoul Tower is open until 22:30 on weekdays and 23:00 on weekends per Visit Seoul, Naksan Park is open 24 hours, and the Banpo Bridge fountain runs 19:30 to 21:00 in October.

Is Naksan Park safe and free to visit at night?

Naksan Park is free and open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week according to Visit Seoul. Seoul Metropolitan Government describes a well-lit trail along the city wall, but take normal night-time care and stay on lit paths.

When does the Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain run?

It operates every day from April to October. Visit Seoul lists 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 and 21:00 evening shows in April to June and September to October, with an extra 21:30 show in July and August. Each show lasts 20 minutes and times can change with the weather.

Is N Seoul Tower worth it at night?

Visit Seoul says digital art is projected onto the tower at night, but the observatory costs 29,000 won for adults and the cable car is 15,000 won round trip. If you only want a free view of the city lights, Naksan Park is the budget alternative.
