Olive Young Shopping Guide for K-Beauty Fans

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 6, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Go to the Olive Young Myeongdong Town store (53 Myeongdong-gil, open 10:00 to 22:30 daily). Staff speak English, Chinese and Japanese, and a purchase of 15,000 won or more with your physical passport qualifies for an immediate tax refund of roughly 5 to 8%. Check each receipt before leaving.

What should I know before I go?

Details checked November 6, 2026

Olive Young is Korea's best-known health and beauty chain, and the Myeongdong Town store is set up for visitors, so bring your passport and plan around the opening hours. Product names in that store appear in Korean and English, according to the Korea JoongAng Daily, and tax refund instructions are available in several languages.

  • Worth knowing: The official Myeongdong Global page lists hours of 10:00 to 22:30 every day, including weekends and holidays, but notes that hours may change.
  • A common mistake: Leaving your passport at the hotel. The in-store refund needs your physical passport, so a photo on your phone is not enough.
  • Local tip: The store has no restroom, so use one at the nearby subway station or a cafe before you start browsing.

Which Olive Young store should I visit?

Start with the Myeongdong Town store, which is the one built for foreign visitors, and treat the ordinary neighbourhood branches as a cheaper, quieter backup. The store is a short walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4, Exit 8) or Euljiro Entrance Station (Line 2, Exit 5), per the official Myeongdong Global page.

How does the tax refund work at Olive Young?

Spend at least 15,000 won on one receipt, show your physical passport at the till, and the VAT is taken off immediately; the refund is roughly 5 to 8% of the purchase. The Myeongdong Global page sets the limits per receipt at 15,000 won to 1,000,000 won, with an overall cap of 5.0 million won, calculated in 1,000 won increments.

  • Not covered: the official page excludes sanitary goods, shopping bags and dried fruit products from the immediate refund.
  • No physical passport: customers without one, those not registered with the Korea Customs Service, or those who have stayed in Korea more than six months can claim at the airport refund counter with a voucher.
  • Other shops: refund slips from other GTF-affiliated stores can be processed at the GTF desk in the Global Service Lounge on the second floor, with your passport.

The national rules are explained in our tax refund guide and the VisitKorea tax refund guide. The three-month export rule still applies, so keep goods unopened until you leave.

How do I shop and claim my refund step by step?

Collect your items, take them to the till with your passport, and confirm the refund on the receipt before you pay.

  1. Bring your passport. Carry the physical document, not a copy.
  2. Browse by zone. The Myeongdong Town store shows a monthly "K-beauty Now" selection near the front, and the CJ newsroom says an English floor map is linked by QR code.
  3. Check the total. Make sure the amount on a single receipt reaches 15,000 won and does not include excluded goods such as shopping bags.
  4. Pay and claim. Show the passport when you pay. For other stores' slips, use the GTF desk on the second floor.
  5. Keep the goods unopened. Customs may check them when you leave Korea.

What should I know about returns, bags and ordering later?

Exchanges are limited to 15 days for unopened regular products, and refunds go back to the store where you bought the item. That is the rule on the Myeongdong Global page, which also lists small extras:

  • Bags: paper bags cost 100 won and reusable bags 500 won.
  • Gift wrapping: 1,000 to 2,500 won depending on size.
  • Payments: the page mentions simple-pay options such as WeChat Pay and Alipay; ask at the till about other methods.
  • Bulk orders: separate bulk orders are not available, so you can only buy what is in stock.
  • Global Service Lounge (2F): free Wi-Fi, relaxation areas and membership gifts.

If something sells out, Olive Young's Global Mall serves customers in 150+ countries, with delivery to major countries in as little as five days according to its corporate global page. Delivery time varies by region.

FAQ

Do I need a passport to shop at Olive Young as a tourist?

You need your physical passport to get the in-store immediate tax refund. Olive Young's Myeongdong Global page says purchases of 15,000 won or more qualify, and without a physical passport you can use the airport voucher route instead.

Which Olive Young store is best for foreign visitors?

The Myeongdong Town store at 53 Myeongdong-gil is the easiest, with English, Chinese and Japanese support, a tax refund desk and a Global Service Lounge. A newer, larger Central Myeongdong Town store was reported in March 2026; confirm its address before you go.

Can I return or exchange products I bought at Olive Young?

The Myeongdong Global page allows exchanges within 15 days for unopened regular products, and refunds only at the store where you bought the item. Tax-refunded goods are a separate matter, so ask staff before you leave.

Can I order from Olive Young after I leave Korea?

Yes. Olive Young runs a Global Mall that its corporate site says reaches 150+ countries, with delivery to major countries in as little as five days, though times vary by region.

Is there a toilet inside the Myeongdong Town store?

According to the store's official page, there is no restroom, and customers are directed to the nearest subway station.
