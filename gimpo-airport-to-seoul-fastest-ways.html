Gimpo Airport to Seoul: The Fastest Ways Into the City

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

About 23 minutes is how long VisitKorea lists the AREX all-stop train from Gimpo Airport to Seoul Station. For Gangnam, Line 9 Express is usually quicker, and Line 5 reaches the Gwanghwamun area. Subway rides use the standard fare of about 1,550 won plus distance charges; taxis are best for groups and late arrivals.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked November 5, 2026

Gimpo is a compact airport with its own subway station, so the train is almost always the fastest way into Seoul. Gimpo mainly handles domestic flights and short regional routes, and it has a Domestic Terminal and an International Terminal.

  • Worth knowing: Gimpo Airport Station serves Subway Lines 5 and 9, the AREX Airport Railroad, the Gimpo Goldline and the Seohae Line, according to Wikipedia. VisitKorea says these lines reach central Seoul within about an hour.
  • A common mistake: Waiting for the AREX Express. Express trains do not stop at Gimpo, so you need an all-stop train.
  • Local tip: Carry a T-money card. Cash is accepted only for certain bus destinations, and international credit cards are not accepted on city buses, per VisitKorea.

Which option is best from Gimpo Airport?

Take the subway or AREX in almost every case; use a bus for destinations the trains miss and a taxi for groups or late arrivals. VisitKorea lists Lines 5 and 9, the Gimpo Goldline and the Seohae Line as the rail links into the city.

  • Going to Hongdae, Digital Media City or Seoul Station: AREX all-stop. It is a direct ride with no transfer to Hongik Univ. Station.
  • Going to Gangnam, Yeouido or Noryangjin: Line 9. Express trains skip many stops, including Yeouido, Noryangjin, Express Bus Terminal and Sinnonhyeon, according to Wikipedia, which says express trains run around five times an hour in each direction.
  • Going to the Gwanghwamun or Jongno area: Line 5, often with a transfer.
  • Hotel off the rail network, or very heavy luggage: a city bus, a limousine bus or a taxi.

Practical example: a traveller heading to a Hongdae guesthouse can ride the AREX all-stop train from Gimpo straight to Hongik Univ. Station. Someone staying near the Gangnam area can take Line 9 Express to Sinnonhyeon or Express Bus Terminal Station instead.

How long and how much does each option take?

Rail trips cost about 1,550 won plus a distance charge and take roughly 25 to 45 minutes to most central areas, while a taxi costs several times more. Times below mix official and secondary sources, so treat them as approximate and confirm on a route planner.

How do I get from the terminal to a train?

Follow signs to Gimpo Airport Station, tap a T-money card and board the line that matches your destination.

  1. Find the station exit. Per Wikipedia, Exit 1 leads to the Domestic Terminal and Exit 2 to the International Terminal.
  2. Get a card. Buy or load a T-money card; see our T-money card guide.
  3. Pick the platform. AREX, Line 5 and Line 9 use separate platforms, and Line 9 has a separate express platform.
  4. Check the train type. On Line 9, confirm whether the next train is express or local before boarding.
  5. Transfer if needed. Change at Digital Media City, Noryangjin or Yeouido depending on where your hotel is.

For buses, VisitKorea says the international terminal limousine platforms are 6 and 7 outside Gate 1, and the domestic terminal platforms are 3, 4 and 5 outside Gate 6. City bus stops are Platform 4 at the international terminal and Platform 2 or 6 at the domestic terminal. Taxi stands are outside Exit 3 (international) and in front of Exit 6 (domestic). The Gimpo Airport Tourist Information Center is at Gate 1 on the first floor of the international terminal, open 10:00 to 19:00 according to VisitKorea.

FAQ

Does the AREX Express go to Gimpo Airport?

No. AREX Express trains do not stop at Gimpo Airport, so you must take an AREX all-stop train, which VisitKorea lists at about 23 minutes to Seoul Station.

Which subway line is best from Gimpo Airport to Gangnam?

Line 9 Express is usually the quickest, reported at about 30 to 35 minutes to Sinnonhyeon; confirm the time with a route planner on the day.

Can I use a T-money card at Gimpo Airport?

Yes. T-money works on the subway and city buses. Cash is accepted only for certain bus destinations and international credit cards are not accepted on city buses, according to VisitKorea.

Is a taxi from Gimpo Airport worth it?

It suits groups, heavy luggage or late arrivals. Use the taxi stands outside Exit 3 (international terminal) or Exit 6 (domestic terminal) and expect a late-night surcharge.
