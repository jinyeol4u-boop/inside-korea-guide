Suwon Hwaseong Fortress Day Trip from Seoul

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Walking Suwon Hwaseong Fortress is free, and Hwaseong Haenggung palace costs about 2,000 won for adults. Take Subway Line 1 from Seoul Station, roughly 50 minutes and 1,650 won, then walk the 5.1 km trail in 2 to 3 hours or ride the tourist trolley. Plan four to six hours in total.

What should I know before I go?

Fees, hours and routes checked November 9, 2026

Treat it as a half-day walk with a palace stop, and check the season because palace hours and the night opening change. The fortress wall is open to walk without a ticket, but the palace, the trolley and the museums have separate hours and fares.

  • Worth knowing: The 2026 Haenggung night opening ran on Fridays to Sundays from May 1 to November 1 per a news report, so it has ended for this year. Expect daytime visits only now.
  • Season note: One tourism page lists palace hours of 09:00 to 18:00 from March to October and 09:00 to 17:00 from November to February, while the foundation lists 09:00 to 18:00. Assume the earlier closing in winter and confirm on the foundation page.
  • Weather: The tourist trolley does not run on rainy or snowy days, so have a walking plan ready.
  • Wear: Comfortable shoes. The wall climbs Paldalsan hill and has steps and slopes.

What is Hwaseong Fortress and what is worth seeing?

Hwaseong is a fortress wall of about 5.7 km built around central Suwon and designated a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage Site on December 12, 1997. VisitKorea describes the wall as circling Paldalsan mountain, and its stone gates, watchtowers and bastions can be seen in sections as you walk.

  • Janganmun (north gate): A good place to start. A tourism guide recommends beginning here and walking the circuit in 2 to 3 hours.
  • Hwaseomun (west gate) and Hwahongmun: Both are stops on the trolley loop, so you can get on and off.
  • Paldalmun (south gate): Sits beside the Paldalmun market streets, handy for lunch.
  • Hwaseong Haenggung: The temporary palace. Visitors in hanbok enter free per the foundation.

Practical example: a first-time visitor with half a day can walk Hwaseomun to Janganmun along the wall, ride the trolley for the rest, and finish at the palace before it closes.

How do I get from Seoul to Suwon?

Take Subway Line 1 to Suwon Station: about 50 minutes from Seoul Station for roughly 1,650 won with a T-money card, with no transfer. These figures come from a travel guide and are approximate, so check the route in a map app before you leave. A T-money card is easiest; see our T-money card guide.

  1. Ride Line 1. Board a train toward Suwon or beyond. Not every train stops at every station, so check the sign on the platform.
  2. Exit Suwon Station. Use the bus stops or taxi rank outside.
  3. Bus or taxi. A local bus or taxi to the fortress area takes roughly 10 minutes. Taxis are listed at around 5,000 to 7,000 won by the same guide, which is an estimate.
  4. Pick your start. Start at Janganmun for the wall, or at Haenggung for the palace first. The palace address is 825 Jeongjo-ro, Paldal-gu, per VisitKorea tips.

KTX and other trains also serve Suwon, but they cost more for a short trip and not every service stops there.

How much do the fortress, palace and trolley cost?

The wall is free, the palace is about 2,000 won for adults, and the trolley is about 6,000 won for adults. Figures below come from the Suwon Cultural Foundation; an older provincial page lists different fortress fees, so treat all prices as approximate.

How should I spend a half day?

Start at Janganmun, walk toward Hwaseomun, have lunch near Paldalmun and end at the palace. VisitKorea lists a 5.1 km trail of 2 to 3 hours with a stamp tour: collect stamps at 7 or more of 10 spots for a souvenir.

  1. Morning: Reach Suwon Station by 09:30 and be at Janganmun by about 10:00.
  2. Midday: Walk or ride to Hwaseomun, then continue toward Paldalmun and its markets for lunch.
  3. Afternoon: Visit Hwaseong Haenggung and allow about an hour, arriving well before closing.
  4. Return: Take a bus or taxi to Suwon Station for Line 1 back to Seoul.

If you only want a short visit, ride the trolley loop of about 20 minutes and spend the rest of your time at the palace.

FAQ

Is Suwon Hwaseong Fortress free to enter?

Walking the fortress wall and gates is free according to the Suwon Cultural Foundation and VisitKorea. Hwaseong Haenggung palace inside the walls charges about 2,000 won for adults.

How long does it take to walk the whole fortress?

VisitKorea lists a 5.1 km walking trail of 2 to 3 hours, while the wall itself is about 5.7 km. A relaxed half day covers it with a stop at the palace.

How do I get to Suwon from Seoul?

Subway Line 1 runs to Suwon Station from Seoul Station in about 50 minutes for roughly 1,650 won with a T-money card. From the station, a local bus or taxi takes about 10 minutes to the fortress area.

Is the Hwaseong Fortress Trolley running in winter?

It is listed by VisitKorea as running Tuesday to Sunday from 09:40 to 17:00 and is closed on rainy or snowy days, so check the foundation's page before you go.
