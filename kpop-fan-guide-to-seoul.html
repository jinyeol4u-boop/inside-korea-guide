A K-Pop Fan's Guide to Seoul: Spots, Shows and Concerts

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 7, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Seoul's free K-pop sights are K-Star Road (about 1 km, with 17 GangnamDol bear statues) and COEX K-Pop Square (listed as open 24 hours). Hongdae adds evening busking. Music show tapings and concerts need applications or tickets, so apply early and confirm every rule on the official page.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 7, 2026

Treat Seoul's K-pop sights as public streets and shops, not backstage access, and expect anything ticketed to need advance booking. Statues move, pop-ups appear for days at a time, and entry rules for tapings change by show.

  • Worth knowing: Gangnam's official K-pop page says the 17 GangnamDol statues "are moved from time to time", so a fixed spot from an older blog may be out of date.
  • A common mistake: Planning around HYBE INSIGHT without checking. The NOL listing for the Yongsan site states it is no longer in operation, and a 2023 report said the museum would reopen in a new space in Samseong-dong, so confirm current status on its official site first.
  • Local tip: Agency offices are working buildings. The Gangnam page says they offer no tours and asks visitors to look from outside and keep noise down.

What can I see for free in Gangnam?

K-Star Road and COEX K-Pop Square are the two free anchors, and both are reachable by subway. Add a nearby shop or class if you want something hands-on.

  • K-Star Road: About 1 km from Apgujeong Rodeo Station (Suin-Bundang Line, Exit 2) toward Cheongdam Crossroads, with 17 bear-shaped GangnamDol statues, each for a K-pop group, per Gangnam's official K-pop page. The district's K-Star Road page confirms the route and the photo-spot statues.
  • COEX K-Pop Square: 513 Yeongdong-daero, Samseong Station (Line 2), Exit 6, listed as free and open 24 hours. Ktown4u COEX on floors 2 to 4 of COEX Artium is listed 11:00-20:00 daily and sells albums and merchandise.
  • Classes: The same page lists MUDOCTOR Academy Gangnam (Mon-Sat 13:00-22:00, closed Sunday) and King Studio Seolleung (daily 10:00-22:00, with English interpretation) for dance sessions. Book through the studio and confirm prices there.

Practical example: a fan arriving by subway can start at Apgujeong Rodeo, walk the road toward Cheongdam, then ride Line 7 and Line 2 to Samseong for COEX in the evening.

Where can I find busking and K-pop shops in Hongdae?

Head to Hongik University Station Exit 9 and the Red Road (Eoulmadang-ro), where a 2026 guide says busking runs from late afternoon, busiest on Friday and Saturday evenings. Hongik University Station is also on AREX, which that guide says takes about 43 minutes from Incheon Airport Terminal 1.

  • Busking: The corridor is described as four zones, with dance crews near the Exit 9 end and quieter acoustic sets toward the river. Hours are informal, so go in the evening.
  • Shops: The same guide lists the WithMuu K-pop store on the 2nd floor of AK Plaza (11:00-22:00) for albums and photocards. Confirm hours before a special trip.
  • Pop-ups: Temporary stores have no central listing. The guide advises checking Weverse and the group's social accounts 48 to 72 hours ahead.

For nearby cafes and districts, see Myeongdong vs Hongdae vs Insadong and our Seongsu-dong guide. The Gangnam page says SM moved to Seongsu in 2021.

How do I attend a K-pop music show taping?

Apply through the broadcaster's official audience page or the artist's fan club notice, expect it to be free but not guaranteed, and bring your original passport. Rules differ by show and change often.

How do I buy concert tickets and find venues?

Use NOL (formerly Interpark Global) if you have no Korean phone number or ID, and set up your account and payment days before sales open. A May 2026 venue guide says dome tickets for major acts can sell out within minutes.

  • KSPO Dome: about 15,000 capacity, Olympic Park Station (Lines 5 and 9), 5 to 10 minutes on foot.
  • Gocheok Sky Dome: about 25,000 capacity, Sindorim Station (Lines 1 and 2), about 5 minutes from Exit 1.
  • INSPIRE Arena (Incheon): about 15,000 capacity, no subway; a free airport shuttle of about 15 minutes, and 1 to 1.5 hours from central Seoul.
  • Fan club presales: Reported to open 24 to 48 hours before general sale. Source: Namanecard venue guide, which is not an official site, so check the artist's notice.

How do I plan a first K-pop day?

Pair one Gangnam stop with one evening Hongdae stop and keep the ticketed items for planned days. The order below avoids crossing the city twice.

  1. Morning in Apgujeong. Walk K-Star Road from Exit 2 toward Cheongdam Crossroads.
  2. Midday at COEX. Visit K-Pop Square and Ktown4u (11:00 to 20:00).
  3. Check pop-ups. Look at the group's Weverse or social posts 48 to 72 hours earlier.
  4. Evening in Hongdae. Reach Exit 9 and watch Red Road busking.
  5. Book separately. Apply for tapings and buy concert tickets through the official routes above.

Practical example: if you land on a Thursday, spend the next two days on free sights and apply for a Friday Music Bank slot early, since results are not guaranteed.

FAQ

Can tourists go inside K-pop agency buildings?

Generally no. Gangnam's official K-pop page describes agency offices such as Starship, FNC, Fantagio and P Nation as working offices without tours and asks visitors to view them from outside and keep noise down.

Is it free to watch a K-pop music show taping?

Broadcaster audience passes are described as free and distributed through applications or lotteries, but approval is not guaranteed. A 2026 guide says fan club allocations can require membership, an album purchase and a lightstick, so read each notice.

Where can I buy K-pop concert tickets as a foreigner?

NOL (formerly Interpark Global) is described as having an English interface and accepting international cards, while the main Interpark site usually needs Korean ID verification. Link your account and payment details days before the sale.

Which K-pop sights are free to visit?

K-Star Road in Apgujeong and COEX K-Pop Square (listed as free and open 24 hours) cost nothing. Hongdae's Red Road busking is also free to watch, mostly in the evening and busiest on Fridays and Saturdays.
