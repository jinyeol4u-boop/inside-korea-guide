Nami Island Day Trip from Seoul: Train, Ferry, Costs

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Nami Island is about 1 to 1.5 hours from Seoul by train plus a 5-minute ferry. Entry is 19,000 won for a regular adult ticket, and the round-trip ferry is included. Take the ITX-Cheongchun or Gyeongchun Line to Gapyeong Station, then walk or ride Bus 10-4 to the wharf.

What should I know before I go to Nami Island?

Fees and schedules checked November 10, 2026

Nami Island sits in a river lake, belongs to Chuncheon in Gangwon Province, and is reached by a short boat ride from Gapyeong in Gyeonggi Province. The official Nami Island page describes a 5-minute ferry ride from Gapyeong Wharf, at 1024 Bukhangangbyeon-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun.

  • Worth knowing: The ticket price already includes the round-trip ferry, according to the official admission page, so you do not buy a separate boat ticket.
  • A common mistake: Heading to the wrong place. Gapyeong Station is in town, not at the water. The wharf is a further 1.5 km on, a walk of about 20 minutes.
  • Local tip: Autumn is the busiest season, so go early. Our autumn foliage guide and November packing guide help with timing and layers.

How do I get from Seoul to Nami Island?

The simplest route is a train to Gapyeong Station followed by a short walk, bus or taxi to Gapyeong Wharf. The official how-to-get-there page lists these train times to Gapyeong Station: Gyeongchun Line from Sangbong about 50 minutes, ITX-Cheongchun from Yongsan about 60 minutes, and ITX-Cheongchun from Cheongnyangni about 42 minutes.

  1. Choose a station. The ITX-Cheongchun stops at Yongsan, Wangsimni and Cheongnyangni; the Gyeongchun Line is the regular commuter line from Sangbong.
  2. Book or tap in. The ITX-Cheongchun is a reserved-seat train; book on the Korail English site or at a station kiosk. See our train booking guide, and a T-money card covers the commuter line.
  3. Get off at Gapyeong Station. The wharf is 1.5 km away, per the official page: about 20 minutes on foot, by Bus 10-4, or about 5 minutes by taxi.
  4. Buy your ticket at the wharf and board the ferry, which takes about 5 minutes.

A third-party train guide lists ITX-Cheongchun fares of about 6,900 won from Yongsan, 5,900 won from Wangsimni and 5,700 won from Cheongnyangni. Treat these as approximate and confirm the current fare on the Korail site.

If you would rather not change trains, the official page lists a daily shuttle bus run by Ktourstory, round trip only. It leaves Hongik University Station Exit 4 at 9:00, Namsan Yejang (near Myeongdong Station) at 9:40 and Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 9 at 9:50, arriving at the island at 11:30, and returns from the island at 16:30. Pets are not allowed on the shuttle.

By car, the official page lists parking at 6,000 won for small vehicles for the first 12 hours, paid by card only, with an extra 1,000 won per hour after 12 hours.

How much does Nami Island cost?

A regular ticket is 19,000 won with the round-trip ferry, and a ticket with a motorboat one way is 29,000 won. The official fee table (listed as the "Round-trip Ferry Fare Included" table) gives these prices:

Children aged 36 months and under enter free. The page lists early-bird tickets (bought and used before 8:00) and evening tickets (bought after 18:31 from April to November, or after 18:01 from December to March) but does not state a price, so check the admission page. It also lists no separate foreigner rate, and it says the island is open throughout the year.

The motorboat is a separate experience: the water attractions page lists a one-way ride from Gapyeong Wharf to Nami Wharf at 10,000 won per person, running 09:00 to 17:30. It gives season dates: the Supercar Boat runs through November 15, and Gapyeong and Nami Wharf service runs until November 29, so late-season visitors should check first.

When do the ferries run?

Ferries run roughly every 10 to 20 minutes during the day, starting at 08:00 and ending at 21:00, with later hours in autumn. The official ferry schedule lists these intervals:

  • 08:00 to 09:00: every 30 minutes.
  • 09:00 to 18:00: every 10 to 20 minutes.
  • 18:00 to 21:00: every 30 minutes.
  • First and last boats: to the island at 08:00 and 21:00; back from the island at 08:05 and 21:05.
  • Autumn 2026 (September 18 to November 22): the first ferry is at 07:30 daily, and the last ferry is at 21:30 on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

The page adds that hours may change and extra ferries may run in peak seasons. If you plan to stay after dark, note the last ferry time back before you cross.

What can I do on the island?

Most visitors walk or cycle the tree-lined paths, with a train, a tour bus and cafes for variety. The official bike center page lists these options and says everything is subject to change with the weather:

  • Bicycle: single bike 5,000 won for 30 minutes or 9,000 won for one hour; couple bike 10,000 won for 30 minutes.
  • Charity Train: 4,000 won one way, from the Song Museum entrance to the central station.
  • Story Tour Bus: 10,000 won for about 20 minutes, boarding near Nami Wharf.
  • Ride around the island: about one hour by bike.

The water park is marked "Closed for the 2026 Season" on the official page, so do not plan a swim. Restaurant and cafe customers get 10% off the boat rides with a receipt, per the same site.

How do the ways to get there compare?

The train is the fastest and cheapest; the shuttle bus is the easiest if you want one seat from central Seoul.

What does a sample day look like?

Leave Seoul by 09:00, spend three to four hours on the island and catch a train back before the evening rush. This outline uses the official times above and is only a suggestion.

  1. 09:00: Board an ITX-Cheongchun or Gyeongchun Line train toward Gapyeong.
  2. 10:00 to 10:30: Arrive, walk or ride Bus 10-4 to the wharf, buy a ticket and cross.
  3. 10:45 to 13:30: Cycle or walk the island, then eat at one of the island restaurants.
  4. 13:30 to 15:00: Take the Charity Train or a cafe break.
  5. 15:00: Ferry back and return to Seoul. Compare with our DMZ tour guide and Suwon day trip if you want another outing.

FAQ

How do I get from Seoul to Nami Island?

Take a train to Gapyeong Station: the ITX-Cheongchun takes about 60 minutes from Yongsan and about 42 minutes from Cheongnyangni, per the official Nami Island site. Then walk 20 minutes, ride Bus 10-4 or take a taxi to Gapyeong Wharf for the 5-minute ferry.

How much does Nami Island cost?

The official fee table lists 19,000 won for a regular ticket, 16,000 won for middle and high school students and seniors aged 70 and over, and 13,000 won for children from 36 months to elementary school age, with the round-trip ferry included. Prices can change, so confirm on the official admission page.

Is Nami Island open in winter?

Yes. The official admission page says it is open throughout the year. Ferries run on a published schedule that changes by season, and the water park is marked closed for the 2026 season, so check the ferry page before you go.

How long should I spend on Nami Island?

Plan about three to four hours on the island. The official bike page says riding around the island takes about one hour, and the Charity Train, Story Tour Bus and cafes can fill the rest of the visit.

Can I visit Nami Island without taking the train?

Yes. A daily shuttle bus operated by Ktourstory leaves Hongik University Station Exit 4 at 9:00, Namsan Yejang at 9:40 and DDP Exit 9 at 9:50, arriving at 11:30, and returns from the island at 16:30. It is round trip only.
