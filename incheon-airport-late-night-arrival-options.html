Arriving at Incheon Late at Night: Your Options

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 9, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

Most public transport from Incheon Airport to central Seoul stops around 23:30. After that, four night buses (N6000, N6002, N6701, N6703) leave Terminal 1 between 23:40 and 04:50 for 17,000 to 18,000 won, taxis add 20% to 40% at night, and the airport also lists capsule hotels for waiting until morning.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 9, 2026

The cheap, regular options end earlier than most arrival times suggest, and the replacement options cost more and run on their own timetable. VisitKorea's airport transportation guide says most public transport from the airport to central Seoul stops around 23:30, and that night buses carry an "N" before the route number. Remember that a flight landing at 22:30 can still mean reaching the bus stop well after midnight once immigration and baggage are done.

  • Worth knowing: The airport's night-bus page covers Terminal 1 in detail and links to a separate page for Terminal 2, so look up the schedule for the terminal you land in.
  • A common mistake: Counting on cash or a transport card. VisitKorea says night buses do not accept either, so you need a ticket from a booth or vending machine first.
  • Local tip: Tell your accommodation your arrival time before you fly and ask whether the front desk is staffed overnight. Our guide to arriving in Korea covers how long the entry steps can take.

What are my options after midnight?

You have three realistic choices: a night bus (17,000 to 18,000 won), a taxi (the most flexible, with a 20% to 40% night surcharge), or waiting at the airport until trains resume. The table compares them using the airport and VisitKorea pages.

Practical example: two friends landing at 23:50 with large suitcases and a hotel in Mapo might split a taxi instead of queueing for the next N-route departure, while a solo traveller going to Gangnam can take the N6000 for 17,000 won.

Which night buses leave from Terminal 1?

Four routes serve Seoul from Terminal 1: N6000 to Gangnam, N6002 to Cheongnyangni, N6701 to Dongdaemun Design Plaza and N6703 to the Gwangnaru area. The figures below come from the airport's Seoul late-night buses page, which says the information is for reference only.

The page lists a child fare of 10,000 won for N6000 and N6002 and 12,000 won for N6701 and N6703, and a 10,000 won adult fare to Songjeong Station on N6000. VisitKorea also notes night buses reach Gyeonggi-do cities such as Goyang, Seongnam and Suwon. The airport says the operators are Airport Limousine (02-2664-9898) for N6000 and N6002 and Korea Airport Limousine (02-2667-0386) for N6701 and N6703. Buses can fill or run late, so treat each time as a guide.

How do I take a night bus step by step?

Buy the ticket first, find the right stop on the 1st floor of Terminal 1, and show the driver your destination. The airport lists the stops below.

  1. Check the schedule. Match your arrival time to the next departure in the table above, and allow for immigration and baggage.
  2. Buy a ticket. Use a ticket booth or vending machine, since cash and transport cards are not accepted on board. For N6701 and N6703, the Tmoney GO app is also listed.
  3. Go to the stop. On the 1st floor of Terminal 1, the airport lists stop 6A for N6000 and N6002, and stop 3B for N6701 and N6703.
  4. Plan the last mile. The buses stop at fixed points, so decide beforehand whether you will walk, or switch to a taxi or the Kakao T app at the drop-off.

How much more does a taxi cost at night?

The airport says night surcharges of roughly 20% to 40% apply between 22:00 and 04:00, depending on the taxi's home region and type. Its taxi guide gives the example that standard taxis from Seoul or Incheon pay 40% from 23:00 to 02:00, while Gyeonggi taxis pay 30% from 23:00 to 04:00.

  • Base fare: 4,800 won for standard taxis and 7,000 won for first-class and oversized taxis, per the airport. For typical daytime totals, see our Incheon to Seoul comparison, then add the surcharge.
  • Out-of-zone trips: The airport says Seoul and Gyeonggi taxis add 20%, and Incheon taxis 30%, for trips beyond Seoul, Incheon, Goyang, Gwangmyeong, Gimpo and Bucheon.
  • Stands: At Terminal 1 the airport lists 5C, 6C and 6D for standard taxis and 4C for international taxis. Confirm on the posted signs, because the page notes that its Terminal 2 table and map do not fully match.
  • Safety: The airport warns that call vans posing as taxis overcharge and solicit illegally, and tells visitors to use designated stands.

Should I wait at the airport until morning?

Waiting makes sense if you land near dawn or your accommodation cannot check you in, and the airport lists capsule hotels for this. The airport has listing pages for Darakhyu at Terminal 1 and Darakhyu at Terminal 2; this guide could not verify their rates or availability, so check those pages before you rely on one.

If you wait, confirm the first AREX train for your terminal with the Airport Railroad (hotline 1599-7788, as given on the airport's AREX page), and keep your T-money card topped up so you can tap straight onto the train. FAQ

Is there a train from Incheon Airport after midnight?

No regular airport train is described as running that late. VisitKorea says most public transport from the airport to central Seoul stops around 23:30, so plan on a night bus or taxi. Confirm the last AREX train for your terminal with the Airport Railroad.

How much is the night bus from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

The airport lists 17,000 won for adults on N6000 and N6002 and 18,000 won on N6701 and N6703 from Terminal 1, with child fares of 10,000 to 12,000 won. Staff and discount tickets are not accepted.

Can I pay for a night bus with cash or a transport card?

VisitKorea says night buses do not accept cash or transportation cards, so buy a ticket in advance at a ticket booth or vending machine. The airport adds that N6701 and N6703 tickets can also be bought through the Tmoney GO app.

Is a taxi more expensive late at night from Incheon Airport?

Yes. The airport says surcharges of roughly 20% to 40% apply between 22:00 and 04:00, depending on the taxi's home region and type. A standard Seoul or Incheon taxi pays 40% from 23:00 to 02:00.

Can I sleep at Incheon Airport until the first train?

The airport lists capsule hotels at both terminals, and its pages describe the terminals as open around the clock. Rates and availability were not verified for this guide, so check the airport's listing before relying on a room.
