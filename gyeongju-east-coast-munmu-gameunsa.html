Gyeongju East Coast: King Munmu's Tomb and Gameunsa

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Five minutes by car separate King Munmu's sea tomb, Gameunsa Temple Site and the Munmu museum on Gyeongju's east coast, and Igyeondae is a 3-minute walk from the museum. Take bus 150 from the express bus terminal stop, or a taxi. Allow a half day, longer with Gampo Port.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 22, 2026

The east coast is a separate half-day trip from Gyeongju's central tombs and temples, so give it its own morning or afternoon. It sits roughly 35 minutes by car from Hwangnidan-gil and Daereungwon, according to the museum's directions page.

  • Worth knowing: Most stops are outdoor and free to see. The newer King Munmu Maritime History Museum is the one staffed site; its museum's visitor page lists winter hours of 10:00 to 17:00, Monday closures and free entry until December 31, 2026.
  • A common mistake: Counting on a city bus for every hop. Sites are a few minutes apart by car but not by bus stop, so a taxi between them saves time.
  • Local tip: This stretch is known for sunrise. The Gyeongju City sunrise guide presents the tomb as Gyeongju's signature New Year sunrise spot, so expect crowds on January 1.

What are the main stops on the east coast?

Five stops cluster within a 15-minute drive: the sea tomb, Gameunsa Temple Site, Igyeondae, Gampo Port and Songdaemal Lighthouse. The distances below come from Gyeongju City's nearby-sites page.

  • Gameunsa pagoda size: A Korea tourism listing gives the pagoda's height as about 13.4 m. Treat it as approximate.

Why is this coast tied to King Munmu?

King Munmu, Silla's king from 661 to 681, asked to be laid to rest in the East Sea so he could guard the kingdom as a sea dragon, and Daewangam is where tradition places his tomb. The city's sunrise guide lists the tomb as State-Designated Historic Site No. 158, Gameunsa Temple Site as No. 31 and Igyeondae as No. 159.

  • Gameunsa: Munmu began the temple, and his son King Sinmun finished it. Today the ruins show twin pagodas and foundations.
  • Igyeondae: Gyeongju's webzine says King Sinmun built it in 681 and that it was rebuilt after excavation found the original site. The Samguk Yusa tells of a magical flute received here.
  • Daewangam debate: The same guide mentions a claim that a rock about 500 m away is the true tomb. Visitors usually treat Daewangam as the accepted site.

How do I get there from central Gyeongju?

Take bus 150, 150-1 or 1150 from the express bus terminal stop to Daejonggyo, then walk about 10 minutes to the museum, or take a taxi. That is the route on the museum's directions page, which also gives 8 minutes by car from the Dong-Gyeongju IC.

  • Distances by car: About 35 minutes from Hwangnidan-gil, Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae or Bomun.
  • Getting to Gyeongju: Read how to book KTX tickets as a foreigner; trains arrive at Singyeongju Station.
  • Taxi tip: Check bus times in a map app first; if the schedule does not suit you, a taxi is the fallback.

What else is worth adding nearby?

If you have extra time, add Jeonchon Dragon Cave, Yangnam Columnar Joints or Girimsa Temple.

  • Jeonchon Dragon Cave: 39 Jangjin-gil, Gampo-eup. The Gyeongju City summer coast guide lists a four-dragon cave and a one-dragon cave, and the Gyeongju City webzine notes it is a sunrise photo spot.
  • Yangnam Columnar Joints: A UNESCO-designated geopark site per the city; its fan-shaped joints became a Natural Monument in 2012, with a 1.7 km seaside path from Haseo to Eupcheon ports.
  • Girimsa Temple: About 19 minutes by car from the museum; the city's page says it was founded in 643 and later renamed by the monk Wonhyo.
  • Coastal trails: The Gampo Kkakji-gil is described as about 80 km around Gampo Port, meeting the Haeparang-gil.

How do I plan the half day?

Start at the museum, walk to Igyeondae, then drive or taxi to the tomb and Gameunsa, and end at Gampo Port.

  1. Check the day: Avoid Mondays for the museum and note the 17:00 winter closing.
  2. Arrive by bus 150: Get off at Daejonggyo and walk 10 minutes.
  3. Museum and Igyeondae: Visit the exhibitions, then walk 3 minutes to the pavilion site.
  4. Tomb and Gameunsa: Take a taxi, 5 minutes each way.
  5. Gampo Port: Finish with the harbour and lighthouse, 15 minutes away, before dark.

FAQ

How do I get from Gyeongju city to King Munmu's tomb without a car?

Gyeongju City's directions page for the museum says to take bus 150 (or 150-1 or 1150) from the express bus terminal stop and get off at Daejonggyo, then walk about 10 minutes. Confirm the timetable on a map app before you go.

Can I walk onto Daewangam, King Munmu's sea tomb?

No. The tomb is a rocky islet in the sea off Bonggil-ri that you view from the shore. It is a designated historic site (Historic Site No. 158) according to Gyeongju City.

Is the Gameunsa Temple Site free to visit?

A Korea tourism listing shows admission as free and the site as open all year, but it is an outdoor ruin without staffed hours. Check the city tourism page if you need certainty.

How long does the Gyeongju east coast take?

Plan a half day for the museum, tomb, Gameunsa and Igyeondae, which are within about five minutes' drive of each other. Add one to two hours for Gampo Port and the lighthouse, or Yangnam's columnar joints.
