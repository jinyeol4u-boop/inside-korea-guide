Where to Stay in Seoul: Best Areas for First-Timers

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 4, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Stay in Myeongdong, Hongdae or near Seoul Station on a first visit, because all three are easy to reach from Incheon Airport. The AREX all-stop train reaches Seoul Station in about 60 minutes for roughly 4,150 to 4,750 won, and Hongdae's Hongik Univ. Station is on the same line.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked October 4, 2026

Seoul's subway links its districts, so your hotel's station and line matter more than its exact neighborhood. Visit Seoul hotel listings say the site lists 350+ hotels and accommodations, with area filters for Gwanghwamun, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun, Hongdae, Itaewon, Yeouido, Gangnam, Jamsil and Seongsu.

  • Worth knowing: Ten or so districts is a lot to compare. Start with two things: how you arrive (see our airport transfer guide) and what you want to do each evening.
  • A common mistake: Booking by price alone. A cheaper room far from a subway line can cost you time and fares every day, so check the walking distance to the nearest station.
  • Local tip: Load a T-money card first, since it works on the subway and city buses (see our subway guide).

Which area of Seoul is best for first-timers?

Myeongdong, Hongdae and the Seoul Station area are the easiest first bases, each for a different reason. Descriptions below come from VisitKorea's Seoul overview; confirm distances on the day.

  • Myeongdong (shopping and central): VisitKorea calls Myeong-dong a representative shopping district with commercial and entertainment venues. It sits on Subway Line 4, the line that also serves Seoul Station.
  • Hongdae (nightlife and youth culture): Near Hongik University and described as the place to feel the most dynamic youth culture. Hongik Univ. Station is on the AREX line, so the airport train runs straight there.
  • Gwanghwamun and Jongno (history): VisitKorea describes the area as full of Korea's history and tradition, with the main palaces and museums. A good fit if sightseeing is your priority.
  • Seoul Station (transit hub): The AREX terminus, with Subway Lines 1 and 4. Useful if you arrive by airport train or plan to take KTX trains onward.
  • Dongdaemun (late-night shopping): Called a major shopping district that does not sleep.
  • Gangnam (trendy and modern): A trendy district where K-pop stars get their makeover and K-beauty trends are on display, per VisitKorea.
  • Itaewon and Seongsu: Itaewon is described as a small global village in Seoul, and Seongsu-dong has a cafe street.

Practical example: a first-time visitor who wants palaces by day and food by night could stay in Myeongdong, since a single subway line connects it to Seoul Station and the center.

What types of places can I stay in Seoul?

Seoul offers hotels, motels, guesthouses and hanok, and the right type depends on budget and comfort. VisitKorea lists hotels from five-star properties to budget business hotels, motels described as cost-effective, guesthouses as relatively inexpensive, and hanok as traditional houses with modern comforts.

  • Hotels: The widest choice. Visit Seoul lists hotels by district, including luxury and mid-range options.
  • Guesthouses and hostels: VisitKorea says guesthouses are relatively inexpensive and offer dormitories, private rooms and social events.
  • Seoul Stay: Seoul Stay refers to accommodations registered with their local district as Urban Stay or Hanok Stay.
  • Hanok: VisitKorea notes that many hanok use mats and blankets on the floor instead of beds, so check bedding before booking.

How do I get to my area from the airport?

The AREX all-stop train is the simplest option for Seoul Station and Hongdae, and airport buses serve other areas. The Visit Seoul Getting to Seoul page lists the all-stop train at about 60 minutes to Seoul Station for 4,150 to 4,750 won, and says buses serve all major areas of Seoul in 60 to 80 minutes depending on traffic.

  • Late arrival: VisitKorea notes that night buses, marked with the letter N, run after 23:30.
  • Taxi: Taxis suit late arrivals or heavy luggage; fares vary by destination, so see our airport transfer guide.
  • Departure day: The Incheon Airport City-Airport Terminal at Seoul Station offers check-in for eligible same-day international departures; check its hours and airlines first.

How do I choose where to stay?

Match the area to your top priority, then check the station distance and bedding before you book.

  1. Set your priority. Shopping points to Myeongdong, nightlife to Hongdae, history to Gwanghwamun and Jongno, onward trains to Seoul Station.
  2. Check airport access. Confirm whether your station is on AREX or whether an airport bus stops nearby.
  3. Check the walk. Measure the walk from the nearest subway exit to the property, especially with luggage.
  4. Check room details. Confirm bedding (bed or floor mat), cancellation terms and the total price on the official or booking page.
  5. Plan your days. Use our Seoul in 3 Days and neighborhoods guide to confirm your base fits the route.

FAQ

What is the best area to stay in Seoul for first-timers?

Myeongdong, Hongdae or the Seoul Station area. VisitKorea calls Myeong-dong a representative shopping district and Hongdae a place for youth culture, and Seoul Station is the AREX airport train terminus.

Is it better to stay in Myeongdong or Hongdae?

Choose Myeongdong for shopping and a central base, or Hongdae for nightlife and a direct AREX ride from Incheon Airport to Hongik Univ. Station. Both are on Seoul's subway network.

Can I stay in a traditional hanok in Seoul?

Yes. Visit Seoul's Seoul Stay listings include registered Hanok Stay properties. VisitKorea warns that many hanok use floor mats and blankets instead of beds, so check the bedding before you book.

Is Gangnam a good place to stay in Seoul?

It suits visitors who want a trendy, modern area. VisitKorea describes Gangnam as where K-pop stars get their makeover and K-beauty trends are on display, but it is south of the Han River and farther from the palaces.

How many hotels does Visit Seoul list?

Visit Seoul says its site lists 350+ hotels and accommodations across districts including Gwanghwamun, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun, Hongdae, Itaewon, Yeouido, Gangnam, Jamsil and Seongsu (checked October 4, 2026).
