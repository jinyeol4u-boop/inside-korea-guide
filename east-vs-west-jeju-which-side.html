East vs West Jeju: Which Side Should You Explore?

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 6, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Pick east Jeju for Seongsan Ilchulbong (5,000 won adult entry), Seopjikoji and Manjanggul; pick west Jeju for Hyeopjae Beach, Hallim Park and sunsets on the Sinchang coast. If you have only one day, choose by interest: volcanic landscapes and sunrise in the east, beaches and sunsets in the west.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 6, 2026

Jeju is large, so most visitors cover one side of the island per day rather than both. Visit Jeju publishes separate suggested trips for the west and the east coast, and both pages say details may change before your trip.

  • Worth knowing: Some east-side sights close for one day a month. Seongsan Ilchulbong is closed on the first Monday and Manjanggul on the first Wednesday, per Visit Jeju and Visit Jeju; the next closures are Wednesday, December 2 and Monday, December 7, 2026.
  • A common mistake: Trying to see both coasts in one day. The sights are spread out, and our Jeju 3-day itinerary plans one region per day for that reason.
  • Local tip: The two Visit Jeju trip pages list no opening hours or prices, so confirm details with the Jeju Tourist Information Center on 064-740-6000.

What is east Jeju best for?

East Jeju is best for volcanic scenery, sunrise and coastal capes. Visit Jeju's six-stop east coast trip runs from Hamdeok to Seongsan and the Aqua Planet aquarium.

  • Seongsan Ilchulbong: Visit Jeju describes a 182-metre volcanic tuff cone with a large crater and a steep but manageable path to the summit. VisitKorea lists adult entry at 5,000 won; see our sunrise hike guide for hours and closing days.
  • Seopjikoji: A cape at Jeju's eastern end with a lighthouse, black stone cliffs and a coastal walking path of about 3.5 km. Visit Jeju lists parking at 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes, with a daily maximum of 3,000 won, and no admission fee.
  • Hamdeok and Woljeong-ri beaches: White sand and turquoise water. Visit Jeju puts Hamdeok about a 40-minute drive from Jeju City, and says Woljeong-ri is a short drive from Manjanggul Cave.
  • Manjanggul and Aqua Planet: Our 3-day itinerary notes Manjanggul's 09:00 to 18:00 hours from Visit Jeju. The east trip page calls Aqua Planet the largest aquarium in Korea.

Practical example: a visitor who wants a sunrise and a quiet cape can climb Seongsan at opening time, then walk the Seopjikoji path before lunch.

What is west Jeju best for?

West Jeju is best for beaches, sunsets and easy coastal drives or walks. Visit Jeju's west trip covers the Aewol coast, Hyeopjae, Hallim Park and the Sinchang windmill road.

  • Hyeopjae Beach: Visit Jeju describes silvery sand, shallow water, a pine grove and a view of Biyangdo island, about 1 km offshore per the west trip page. The beach is open at all times; lifeguard hours of 10:00 to 19:00 apply from June 24 to September 6, 2026. A parasol is listed at 20,000 won.
  • Hallim Park: Visit Jeju says it opened in 1971 and has nine themed areas across 330,000 square metres. Ticket sales run 09:00 to 16:30 from November to January, shifting with sunset; prices are not listed, so call 064-796-0001.
  • Aewol Coastal Road: About 10 km of cliffs, black volcanic beaches and cafes that you can drive, cycle or walk as Olle Trail Route 16. For routes, see our Olle Trail guide for beginners.
  • Sinchang Windmill Coastal Road: An offshore wind farm and a walking promenade over the water. Visit Jeju calls it a good sunset spot and warns that it can be very windy.

Practical example: a beach-and-sunset day can start at Hyeopjae, visit Hallim Park a few hundred metres away, then end on the Sinchang coast as the light fades.

How do I choose, and how do I get around?

Choose the side that matches your main interest, then use the 810 or 820 shuttle or a taxi for the stops buses reach poorly. Our Jeju without a car guide cites the official guidebook for both shuttles running about every 30 minutes, and gives a day pass of about 3,000 won; see Visit Jeju's transport page for current options.

  1. Decide your top priority. Sunrise and volcanic views point east; beaches and sunsets point west.
  2. Check closing days. Avoid the first Monday for Seongsan and the first Wednesday for Manjanggul.
  3. Pick your transport. Use the 810 for the east, the 820 for the west, or a taxi or tour for a tight schedule.
  4. Plan one side per day. If you have three days or more, give each coast its own day; start from our Jeju 3-day itinerary.

In November the days are short, so Hallim Park's ticket office closes at 16:30 and a west-side sunset day needs an early start.

FAQ

Is east or west Jeju better for a first visit?

East Jeju suits a first visit if you want Seongsan Ilchulbong and the Seopjikoji cape; west Jeju suits beach and sunset plans. Both can be seen by bus, but they are best done as separate days because the island is large.

Where is the best place to watch sunrise and sunset on Jeju?

Seongsan Ilchulbong on the east coast is the classic sunrise spot, and Visit Jeju describes the Sinchang windmill coastal road in the west as a good place for sunsets.

Is Hyeopjae Beach free to enter?

Visit Jeju's page lists no entry fee and says the beach is open at all times. Rentals cost extra: a parasol is listed at 20,000 won and a wooden platform at 30,000 won for 2026.

Can I visit east and west Jeju in one day?

It is possible by car or tour, but it means a long drive across the island. The Jeju tourist information line, 064-740-6000, can help you check current routes and times.

Can I see both sides without renting a car?

Yes, to a degree. The 810 tourist shuttle serves the east and the 820 the west, each about every 30 minutes, per the official 2024 guidebook as cited in our Jeju without a car guide.
