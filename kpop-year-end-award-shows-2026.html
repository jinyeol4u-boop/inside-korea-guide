K-Pop Year-End Award Shows: Dates and How to Watch

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Four big K-pop year-end shows are on the 2026-27 calendar. Melon (Nov 14-15, Seoul) and MAMA (Nov 20-21, Osaka) are over. The Asia Artist Awards run December 5-6 in Taiwan and Golden Disc is January 9-10, 2027 in Hanoi. None of the remaining two is in Korea.

What should I know before I arrive?

Details checked November 22, 2026

The big K-pop year-end ceremonies no longer all happen in Seoul, so check the city before you book a flight to Korea for one. This season only the Melon Music Awards were in Korea, and they have finished.

  • Worth knowing: Organizers move venues between years. Golden Disc is in Hanoi in 2027, which JoongAng Daily describes as the eighth city outside Korea to host it.
  • A common mistake: Assuming a ceremony needs a ticket to enjoy. Most have worldwide livestreams, and AAA's was reported as a Disney+ exclusive.
  • Local tip: Korea's own year-end TV music festivals are a separate set of broadcasts. Their dates were not verified for this guide, so check the broadcasters before relying on them.

Which K-pop award shows are happening this season?

Two have finished and two are ahead: Asia Artist Awards on December 5-6, 2026 and Golden Disc Awards on January 9-10, 2027.

  • MAMA: Wikipedia lists the ceremony schedule as November 20 and 21, with the red carpet and main ceremony each day, and the organizer as CJ ENM through Mnet.
  • AAA: The red carpet starts at 3 p.m. and the ceremony at 5 p.m. Taiwan time on both days, per Starnews. Taiwan is one hour behind Korea.
  • Golden Disc: JoongAng Daily reported an outdoor special stage in Hanoi over two days.

How can I watch each award show?

For AAA, use Disney+; for the other shows, confirm the platform on the organizer's page, because streaming partners change every year.

  1. AAA: Starnews reported on November 6, 2026 that Disney+ will stream the ceremony exclusively worldwide, with a one-hour delayed broadcast on Taiwan's SETTV. An earlier IBTimes Singapore article from May said streaming was not yet announced, so the newer report is the one to follow.
  2. Golden Disc: The sources read for this guide gave dates, venue and hosts but no broadcast or streaming partner. Treat it as not yet announced and check the organizer closer to January.
  3. MAMA (for reference): Wikipedia lists the 2026 broadcast through Mnet, tvN Show, TVING, Naver TV, CHZZK, Mnet Plus and several YouTube channels, which shows how many platforms one show can use. The ceremony has already ended.
  4. Time zones: Korea is UTC+9. A 5 p.m. Taiwan start is 6 p.m. in Korea, so convert before setting an alarm.

Can I attend an award show in person?

Only sometimes: seats are limited, and AAA's 100,000 seats were reported sold out on November 6, 2026. For Golden Disc, no ticket sale details were found in the sources read.

  • Official channels only: Buy from the organizer's named ticket platform. For MMA2026 that was Melon Ticket.
  • Invitation draws: The Korea Times noted a "Catch the Timing" promotion with 224 invitations to win, so some shows hand out seats by draw.
  • Music shows instead: If you are in Seoul and want a live K-pop experience, our K-pop fan's guide to Seoul covers music show applications, concerts and fan spots.

What did MMA2026 teach us about tickets and timing?

The Melon Music Awards used a tiered presale where only long-time Melon members got first access, and each Melon ID could buy two tickets in total. The Sports Kyunghyang described the rules, which are a useful template for next year.

  • Lesson for next year: The first round opened about 26 days before the show, so account status can matter months ahead. Check the Melon site for the next edition.

How do I plan a trip around an awards show?

Confirm the city, streaming platform and ticket rules first, and only then book flights and a hotel.

  1. Pick the show: Check the table above for date and venue, since two of the four were outside Korea.
  2. Check streaming: If you only want to watch, a Disney+ account for AAA may be all you need.
  3. Check tickets: Look for the organizer's official ticket platform and its sales dates, then compare with your travel dates.
  4. Build in flexibility: Choose refundable flights and hotels until your seat or livestream plan is confirmed.
  5. Add Korea time: If you are already visiting Korea in winter, see our winter festivals guide for other events.

FAQ

Which K-pop year-end award shows are still ahead?

As of November 22, 2026, the Asia Artist Awards run December 5 and 6 at Kaohsiung National Stadium in Taiwan, and the 41st Golden Disc Awards run January 9 and 10, 2027 in Hanoi, Vietnam. The Melon Music Awards and MAMA have already taken place.

Where can I watch the Asia Artist Awards 2026?

Starnews reported on November 6, 2026 that Disney+ will stream the ceremony exclusively worldwide. In Taiwan there is also a one-hour delayed broadcast on SETTV. Check Disney+ in your country for availability.

Are any of these ceremonies held in Korea this year?

Not among the four checked here. The Melon Music Awards were at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul on November 14 and 15, but MAMA was in Osaka, AAA is in Taiwan and Golden Disc is in Hanoi. Korean TV year-end music festivals were not checked for this guide.

Can foreign visitors buy tickets to award shows?

Ticketing is set by each organizer. For MMA2026, Melon Ticket ran two presales and limited each Melon ID to two tickets. Check the organizer's page and Melon, and be wary of resale sites.
