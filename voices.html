Community guidelines

  • Share your own experience. Be honest, specific and kind.
  • No hate speech, harassment, sexual content, violence or discrimination of any kind.
  • No advertising, links, contact details or personal information about other people.
  • Respect privacy: do not name private individuals or businesses you are complaining about.
  • English is preferred so every reader can follow along.

Every submission is reviewed before it appears, usually within a few days. Stories that break these guidelines are not published, and we may remove published stories at any time. Stories reflect the views of their authors, not of Inside Korea Guide. See our Terms of Use.
