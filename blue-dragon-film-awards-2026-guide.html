Blue Dragon Film Awards 2026: Korea's Top Movie Night

By Dragon Kim · Updated November 25, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

The 47th Blue Dragon Film Awards are set for November 25, 2026 at KBS Hall in Yeouido, Seoul, with a KBS2 broadcast, according to Korean news. Hope leads with 10 nominations. The reports give no start time or public ticket details, so check the KBS schedule.

What should I know before I follow the awards?

Details checked November 25, 2026

The ceremony is scheduled for the same day this guide was written, so winners were not yet known when we checked. Our facts come from Korean news published on October 28, 2026, so treat times and platforms as unconfirmed until you see the broadcaster's own listing.

  • Worth knowing: Nominee names and film titles below come from Korean reports; English titles are taken from Wikipedia and may differ from the titles used on release in your country.
  • A common mistake: Assuming tourists can buy seats. The reports we read mention no ticket sales, so do not plan a trip around attending.
  • Local tip: The ceremony is a TV event. Korean dates and times use KST, which is UTC+9, so convert before you set a reminder.

What are the Blue Dragon Film Awards?

They are an annual Korean film awards ceremony; the 2026 edition is the 47th. Wikipedia describes it as an award for excellence in Korean cinema, and Korean news refers to it as the 제47회 청룡영화상. The 2026 eligibility window, per WikiTree, covers films released from October 8, 2025 to October 11, 2026, including films released on streaming services, and 141 films were in the preliminary pool.

  • Venue: IS Plus reports that the ceremony is held at KBS Hall in Yeouido and relayed on KBS2.
  • Hosts: Not stated in the reports we read.
  • Organizer: We could not confirm this on an official page, so we do not name one here.

Who are the 2026 nominees?

Five films are nominated for Best Film: Colony, The World of Love, The Assassin(s), The King's Warden and Hope. This follows the nominee lists published on October 28, 2026 by Etoday, Star News and IS Plus; English titles are from Wikipedia.

How are winners chosen, and can fans vote?

Winners are decided by expert voting, and fans take part through an online vote in an app. According to the Korean reports, expert voting ran from October 13 to October 25, 2026, and the fan vote opened on November 2 in the Celeb Champ (셀럽챔프) mobile app.

  • Weight of the fan vote: Etoday and Star News say the netizen vote counts the same as one professional juror's vote, across 16 categories including Best Film, Director, Actor and Actress.
  • Is it still open? The articles do not say when the fan vote closes. Check the app or the official site before relying on it.

How can I watch or follow the night?

KBS2 is the named broadcaster; the Korean reports describe it as a relay (중계), and the start time was not stated. Check the KBS programme guide for the live slot, and look for the result lists published by Korean news soon after.

  • Outside Korea: We could not verify an official streaming platform. Wikipedia lists KBS, YouTube and Naver NOW as platforms, but that is not an official statement.
  • Seats at the hall: No ticket sales or public attendance were mentioned in the sources we read.
  • After the night: If you cannot watch live, search the winners' names on the official site or in Korean news once the results are published.

FAQ

When are the 47th Blue Dragon Film Awards?

Korean news reports from October 28, 2026 say the ceremony is on November 25, 2026 at KBS Hall in Yeouido, Seoul, with a KBS2 broadcast. The articles we read did not give a start time, so check the KBS2 schedule.

Can tourists attend the Blue Dragon Film Awards ceremony?

The articles we read mention no public ticket sales or seating for the ceremony. The only public role they describe is an online fan vote in the Celeb Champ app. Check the awards' official site for any audience notice.

Which films have the most nominations in 2026?

Korean reports list Hope with 10 nominations, Possible Love with 8, The King's Warden with 7 and Colony with 6. English titles come from Wikipedia and may differ from official release titles.

Can I watch the Blue Dragon Film Awards outside Korea?

The articles we read name KBS2 as the broadcaster and do not name a streaming platform. Wikipedia lists KBS, YouTube and Naver NOW as platforms, but we could not confirm this on an official page. Check KBS before the night.
