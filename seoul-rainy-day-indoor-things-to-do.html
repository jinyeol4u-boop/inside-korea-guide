Seoul on a Rainy Day: 15 Indoor Things to Do

By Dragon Kim · Updated October 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Seven of the 15 indoor options below cost nothing to enter, including the National Museum of Korea, the War Memorial of Korea and the Seoul Museum of History. Most museums close on Mondays, and several stay open until 21:00 on Wednesdays, Fridays or Saturdays. Confirm each venue's hours before you go.

What should I know before I arrive?

Information checked October 22, 2026

Plan around closing days first, because many Seoul museums close on Mondays and some rooms close for works. Visit Seoul's rainy-day museum list notes that its details were current as of October 9, 2026 and may change.

  • Worth knowing: The National Museum of Korea and several other national museums stay open late on Wednesdays and Saturdays, which suits an evening in the rain.
  • A common mistake: Trusting an old list. Visit Seoul says the National Hangeul Museum is closed for renovation until the second half of 2028, and the National Folk Museum of Korea says Permanent Exhibition Hall 1 is closed from September 30 to December 21, 2026.
  • Local tip: Choose places that sit close to a subway exit. Most venues below list a nearest station on their Visit Seoul pages, and our transit pass guide explains tourist passes.

Which free museums are best in the rain?

Start with the large national and city museums, because each lists free admission for its main displays. Items 1 to 6 below are all free as of the dates on their official pages.

  1. National Museum of Korea. Free for the permanent exhibition; special exhibitions are charged. Open 9:30 to 17:30, or to 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays, per the museum. Ichon Station (Line 4), Exit 2, is 309 m away per Visit Seoul, which lists nearly 420,000 artifacts.
  2. War Memorial of Korea. Free, open Tuesday to Sunday 09:30 to 17:40, and closed Mondays, per Visit Seoul. It has six exhibition halls and about three hours of content; Samgakji Station (Line 4) Exit 11 is 227 m away.
  3. Seoul Museum of History. Free, closed Mondays, with Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5) Exit 7 about 462 m away. Visit Seoul lists extended hours until 21:00 on several days from September 15 to November 30, 2026 (details).
  4. National Folk Museum of Korea. Free; March to October hours are 09:00 to 18:00 (to 20:00 on Wednesdays), per the museum. Remember the Hall 1 closure noted above.
  5. National Museum of Korean Contemporary History. Free, Gwanghwamun Station Exit 2, a 3-minute walk, per Visit Seoul. Hours are 10:00 to 18:00, or to 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays.
  6. National Palace Museum of Korea. Free, Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3) Exit 5, a 2-minute walk. It closes on the last Monday of each month, per Visit Seoul.

For more no-cost ideas, see our free things to do in Seoul guide.

Where can I see art when it rains?

Four art venues cover most budgets, from a free city museum to a combined-ticket private museum. Prices below come from the Visit Seoul pages linked in each item.

  1. Seoul Museum of Art (Seosomun). Listed as free on Visit Seoul's rainy-day page; closed Mondays. Large exhibitions may charge, so confirm on the museum site. Two Visit Seoul pages give different City Hall Station exits, so check the exit in your map app.
  2. National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (MMCA) Seoul. Open 10:00 to 18:00, or to 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays; Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 1 is about 764 m away, per Visit Seoul. The standard fee was not listed, so check the MMCA site.
  3. MMCA Deoksugung. 2,000 won, plus 1,000 won for Deoksugung Palace entrance; closed Mondays, per Visit Seoul. City Hall Station Exit 1 is a 4-minute walk.
  4. Leeum Museum of Art. The M1 Korean antique art gallery is free; a combined ticket is 25,000 won. Open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00 and closed Mondays, per Visit Seoul. Hangangjin Station Exit 1 is 399 m away.

What can I do indoors that is not a museum?

Aquariums, a giant public library and a bathhouse give you dry options with a different pace. Fees and hours change, so verify before you go.

  1. Seodaemun Prison History Hall. Entry is 3,000 won for adults (ages 19 to 64), per Visit Seoul. March to October hours are 09:30 to 18:00, it closes Mondays, and Dongnimmun Station (Line 3) Exit 5 is 209 m away. Our judgment: parts of the site, such as the watchtower, are outdoors, so bring a rain cover.
  2. Lotte World Aquarium. Adult tickets are 35,000 won and child tickets 31,000 won. Hours are 10:00 to 20:00 Monday to Thursday and 10:00 to 22:00 Friday to Sunday, per Visit Seoul. Jamsil Station Exit 10 is 14 m away, and the same page says about 55,000 animals live there.
  3. Starfield Library (COEX Mall). Free, open daily 10:30 to 22:00, and listed as open year round on Visit Seoul. Samseong Station (Line 2) Exit 5 is 353 m away.
  4. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP). Visit Seoul lists hours of 10:00 to 20:00 varying by space, and Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 1 is about 37 m away. It gave no admission fee, so check the DDP site for the exhibition you want.
  5. Jjimjilbang. A Korean bathhouse is a classic wet-day choice; read our jjimjilbang guide for etiquette and how to use one.

Gwangjang Market, covered in our market food guide, is another place to eat out of the weather.

How do I plan a rainy day in Seoul?

Pair two or three venues near the same subway line, and keep the walking short. This is our suggestion, not an official itinerary.

  1. Pick your area. Gwanghwamun has the Seoul Museum of History (Line 5, Exit 7), the Contemporary History museum (Exit 2) and the Seoul Museum of Art in City Hall, based on the exits above.
  2. Check closing days. Look up Monday closures and any works on each official page before you leave your hotel.
  3. Add a long-visit anchor. The National Museum of Korea or War Memorial of Korea each fill several hours; Visit Seoul puts the War Memorial at about three hours.
  4. Finish with food. End at a covered market or a cafe; see Seoul neighborhoods to match an area to your plans.

Practical example: for a Wednesday, spend the afternoon at the National Museum of Korea, which stays open until 21:00, and eat nearby after you leave.

FAQ

Which indoor attractions in Seoul are free on a rainy day?

The National Museum of Korea (permanent exhibition), War Memorial of Korea, Seoul Museum of History, National Folk Museum of Korea, National Museum of Korean Contemporary History, National Palace Museum of Korea and Starfield Library list free admission. Special exhibitions can charge, so check first.

Are Seoul museums open on Mondays?

Often not. Visit Seoul lists the War Memorial of Korea, Seoul Museum of History, Leeum Museum of Art and Seodaemun Prison History Hall as closed on Mondays. Plan museum days for Tuesday to Sunday and keep a Monday backup such as Starfield Library, which Visit Seoul lists as open daily.

What can I do in Seoul at night when it rains?

The National Museum of Korea is open until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and the Seoul Museum of History stays open late on some days through November 30, 2026. Starfield Library lists 22:00 closing and Lotte World Aquarium lists 22:00 on Fridays to Sundays.

Do I need to book tickets for indoor attractions?

Sources checked do not require advance booking for the free permanent exhibitions. Lotte World Aquarium and paid exhibitions may sell tickets online, so check each official page. We could not verify booking rules for every venue.
